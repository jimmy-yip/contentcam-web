# ContentCam changelog: every update for iPhone and Android

Source: https://contentcam.app/changelog/

Every ContentCam update, newest first: iPhone (iOS 2.9, November 2026) and Android (2.7.1, November 2026). A new update every 1 to 2 weeks on each platform: 30 iPhone and 17 Android releases since May 2026.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Is ContentCam still being updated? Yes: **a new update every 1 to 2 weeks on each platform**. That is 30 iPhone releases since the first one on 14 May 2026, and 17 Android releases since the Google Play launch on 8 June 2026. The current versions are **iOS 2.9** (12 November 2026) and **Android 2.7.1** (November 2026). Every update is listed below, newest first. ContentCam is built by Jimmy, a solo developer in Sydney, who reads every feature request and bug report.

## iPhone

### iOS 2.9 · 12 November 2026

- Apple Log: record flatter footage with more detail for colour grading, on iPhone 15 Pro and newer Pro models.
- Dual Lens 4K: the vertical video can now record in 4K on supported iPhones.
- Front & Back 4K: the back camera can record in 4K, vertical, on supported iPhones.
- The telephoto lens is out of beta.
- Rolling out gradually over 7 days from 12 November.

### iOS 2.8.3 · 11 November 2026

- The grid and crosshair show on every preview.
- In Single Lens with the layout swapped, the big view takes tap-to-focus and pinch-to-zoom.
- Select All in the library, and a clearer timelapse label.

### iOS 2.8.2 · 6 November 2026

- Maintenance and polish across recording and the library.

### iOS 2.8.1 · 4 November 2026

- Maintenance and polish across recording and the library.

### iOS 2.8 · October 2026

- Combine: turn a Front & Back recording into one video, as picture-in-picture or split screen.
- PAL formats: record at 25 or 50 fps (turn them on in Settings).
- Teleprompter scripts can be up to 15,000 characters.

### iOS 2.7 · October 2026

- Pause and resume while recording: one recording, with the paused part removed.

### iOS 2.6.1 · October 2026

- Telephoto lens (BETA) on iPhones whose telephoto is longer than 2×.
- Step to the next or previous recording with arrows.
- Select several recordings and share or save them in one go, trims included.

### iOS 2.6 · September 2026

- Choose the folder each project's recordings go into.
- Move recordings between scenes and projects, and reorder your library.
- Now in French, Japanese and Indonesian: eight languages in all.

### iOS 2.5.1 · September 2026

- Fix for front-camera photos on iPhone 17 models.

### iOS 2.5 · September 2026

- Photo mode: one press saves a 9:16 and a 16:9 photo of the same moment.
- AE/AF lock: press and hold the viewfinder.
- Fully translated into German.

### iOS 2.4.1 · August 2026

- Audio fixes: fewer dropouts, better sync, and sound that resumes after a call or Siri.
- More moments to leave a rating in the app.

### iOS 2.4 · August 2026

- Front & Back records both cameras at 60fps.
- Three sizes for the smaller preview.
- Select and delete several takes at once.
- Fully translated into Spanish.

### iOS 2.3.2 · August 2026

- Fixes: audio dropouts and drift, 60fps that recorded at 30fps, a record button that could hang, and a landscape preview that could freeze.

### iOS 2.3.1 · August 2026

- Fix for a launch crash on Pro Max models, and sturdier camera setup.

### iOS 2.3 · August 2026

- Steadier footage in every mode, and true 10-bit HDR recording.
- The ultra-wide 0.5x lens works on the back camera in Front/Back; tap to focus on the scene tile.
- Front-camera mirroring setting, start and stop tones, countdown torch flash, front-camera zoom.

### iOS 2.2 · August 2026

- Front & Back mode: record the selfie and back cameras together, as two separate files.
- The back camera can record vertical or horizontal.
- Front-camera preview and audio-meter fixes on iPhone 17 models.

### iOS 2.1 · July 2026

- Portrait-only recording: turn the landscape file off and use ContentCam like a regular camera.
- Dual Lens: zoom the landscape on its own mid-recording.
- Centre crosshair that lights up cyan when the phone is flat.

### iOS 2.0.1 · July 2026

- Reliability fixes across recording and review.

### iOS 2.0 · July 2026

- Built-in teleprompter: scroll your script hands-free while you record, with adjustable speed, size, colour, mirroring and reading line.
- Monthly and annual plans alongside the one-time lifetime purchase.

### iOS 1.5.1 · June 2026

- White balance with temperature and tint.
- Fixes for recordings that could start with missing audio or a stretched length.

### iOS 1.5 · June 2026

- Swap the main preview with a tap.
- Manual settings are remembered between sessions.

### iOS 1.4.4 · June 2026

- Native-grade stabilisation in single, dual and timelapse.
- Faster lens, resolution and camera switching; 4K and Photos-ordering fixes.

### iOS 1.4.3 · June 2026

- Stabilisation on by default.

- Bigger landscape preview, calmer zoom dial, and a crash fix.

### iOS 1.4.2 · May 2026

### iOS 1.3 · May 2026

### iOS 1.2 · May 2026
