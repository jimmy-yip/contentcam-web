Quick answers

The usual questions

Does recording both formats halve the quality?

No. Both files encode independently in HEVC: portrait at 1080×1920 and landscape at 1920×1080, or 4K equivalents.

Do I need a subscription?

No. The free tier is free forever. Premium is optional: an annual plan with a free trial, a monthly plan, or a one-off lifetime purchase.

Can it record the front and back cameras at the same time?

Yes, on iPhone and Android. Front & Back is a premium mode with 5 free recordings to try.

Which phones does it support?

iPhone XS and later on iOS 18+. Dual Lens mode needs iPhone 11 or later. On Android, any phone with a capable camera. 4K and manual exposure depend on the hardware. What's different on Android →

Is there a watermark?

Never, on any tier, free included.
