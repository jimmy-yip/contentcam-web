## How ContentCam compares with other apps

- [**ContentCam vs SplitRig**](https://contentcam.app/blog/splitrig-vs-contentcam/)
Free, iOS 26 only, one combined video at 24 or 30fps. ContentCam saves two separate files, up to 60fps on iPhone, plus Android.
- [**ContentCam vs ReFrame**](https://contentcam.app/blog/reframe-vs-contentcam/)
Portrait and landscape only, iPhone only, contains ads. ContentCam adds Front & Back, Dual Lens, manual controls and Android.
- [**ContentCam vs Apple Dual Capture**](https://contentcam.app/vs/apple-dual-capture/)
Built in on iPhone 17 and later, one combined video at up to 30fps. ContentCam works on every iOS 18 iPhone, including 15 and 16, as two files.
- [**ContentCam vs DoubleTake**](https://contentcam.app/blog/doubletake-vs-contentcam/)
No update since March 2025, 1080p cap, weekly subscriptions. ContentCam is updated every 1 to 2 weeks.
- [**ContentCam vs DualShot Recorder**](https://contentcam.app/blog/dualshot-recorder-vs-contentcam/)
Portrait and landscape on iPhone, one camera at a time, watermark on free. ContentCam adds Front & Back and Android, no watermark.
- [**ContentCam vs Dualgram**](https://contentcam.app/vs/dualgram/)
Free with ads and a watermark, iPhone only. ContentCam has no ads or watermark on any plan, and runs on Android too.
- [**ContentCam vs MixCam**](https://contentcam.app/vs/mixcam/)
Weekly subscription, watermark removal sold separately. ContentCam: two separate files, no watermark, lifetime option.
- [**ContentCam vs 2Cam**](https://contentcam.app/vs/2cam/)
Front and back recorder that sells watermark removal. ContentCam adds vertical and horizontal from one take, with no watermark.
- [**ContentCam vs Lookout (KIDA Studios)**](https://contentcam.app/vs/lookout/)
Front and back recorder from KIDA Studios. ContentCam saves two separate files and runs on iPhone and Android.
- [**ContentCam vs Detail**](https://contentcam.app/vs/detail/)
Multi-camera recorder with an AI editor, iPhone and Mac. ContentCam runs on iPhone and Android, with no watermark and a lifetime option.
- [**ContentCam vs DualCam**](https://contentcam.app/vs/dualcam/)
iPhone only, 9:16 and 16:9 in one take. ContentCam adds Android and saves front and back as two separate files.
- [**ContentCam vs Blackmagic Camera**](https://contentcam.app/blog/blackmagic-camera-vs-contentcam/)
A pro manual camera, one orientation at a time. ContentCam records vertical and horizontal from one take, and front and back together.
