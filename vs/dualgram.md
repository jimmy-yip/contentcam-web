## What happened when I tested Dualgram 2.5?

I installed Dualgram 2.5 (build 1224) from the App Store on 12 November 2026 and tried every setting it has, on the free version and against the PRO prompts. This is what I found.

- **4K with both cameras: no.** 4K at 60 fps shows "Oops... the selected resolution/FPS is not supported." on the back and the front camera. 4K at 30 fps on both cameras shows "Your device may not support this resolution and frame rate."
- **60 fps with both cameras: the front camera drops to 720p.** At 1080p 60 fps on both, the settings sheet warns the device may not support it, and once the cameras run the app shows "Device overloaded! Reducing front camera resolution to 720." The saved video was 1080 x 1920, HEVC, at 58.91 fps.
- **Cinematic stabilisation lags the preview.** The app warns "Video preview will lag significantly", and the picture on screen ran about 2 seconds behind. Dualgram's user guide says the lag is in the live preview only, with no "lag (or de-sync) in the recorded video file", but it makes framing a moving shot hard. ContentCam runs cinematic stabilisation with a live preview on the main view and the picture-in-picture.
- **Saving the two camera files separately is PRO.** The free version saves the combined video only. "Unlock PRO to save the rear camera video separately", and the same for the front.
- **The free version has ads and a watermark.** The watermark is a large "Shot with Dualgram" text that sits right above the picture-in-picture window, where your face is.
- **Layouts and moving the window are PRO.** Picking another layout shows "Unlock PRO to use this camera layout." The picture-in-picture window can't be resized: pinching does nothing and there is no size button.
- **The teleprompter doesn't scroll.** It is a PRO feature, the text sits still on screen with no speed, size or colour settings, and you scroll it with your finger while you talk.
- **No focus or exposure controls.** Tapping the preview doesn't focus, there is no AE/AF lock, and zoom is a 1x / 0.5x switch. Frame rates are 24, 30 or 60 fps, with no 25 or 50.
- **A force-closed take was lost.** When I closed the app mid-recording the video wasn't saved, and locking the phone mid-take froze the app. ContentCam keeps recording through auto-lock, and recovers a force-quit take on the next launch.
- **No projects or takes.** Videos go straight to Photos as DGC_ files, with nothing to sort them by shoot.
Dualgram 2.5 with both cameras on 4K 60 fps: "not supported" on each. Both cameras at 1080p 60 fps: Dualgram cuts the front camera to 720p. Cinematic stabilisation lags the preview; 4K 30 fps on both cameras gets a warning. What Dualgram PRO unlocks: separate camera files, layouts, microphone options, the teleprompter, and no watermark or ads.

What Dualgram does well: it saves a finished picture-in-picture or split clip straight out of the camera, so there is nothing to put together afterwards, PRO saves the two originals as well, and you can pick the microphone. PRO comes with a one-week free trial, as a yearly plan or a one-time lifetime unlock. If you want one finished clip, framed while you film, it does that job. If you want 4K, 60 fps from both cameras, a teleprompter that scrolls, or your layout decided later, it doesn't.

## Does Dualgram record 4K with both cameras?

Not in my test. On 12 November 2026, Dualgram 2.5 showed "the selected resolution/FPS is not supported" for 4K at 60 fps on both cameras, and "Your device may not support this resolution and frame rate" for 4K at 30 fps on both. Its App Store listing says "Support up to 4k, 60 fps", and its user guide says the front and back cameras can each be set "up to 4K resolution and 60 FPS", adding: "Depending on the iPhone model, certain configurations would not be supported, so you have to try out to know." (checked 11 November 2026). Apple only lets third-party apps record two cameras in formats it marks as multi-camera capable. On an iPhone 18 Pro that list includes 3840 x 2160 at 30 fps but no 4K format at 60 fps, so 4K at 60 fps can't come from two cameras recording at once. ContentCam's Front & Back records in 4K on supported iPhones (from iOS 2.9), as two separate files.
