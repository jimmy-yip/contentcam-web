ContentCam vs Lookout by KIDA Studios (2026)

Checked 6 November 2026 · Disclosure: I'm Jimmy and I build ContentCam

Pick ContentCam over Lookout if you want the front and back camera as two separate files you can edit, a vertical and a horizontal video from the same take, manual ISO, shutter and white balance, or an Android phone. Lookout Dual Camera Recorder by KIDA Studios records both iPhone cameras at once and runs on iPhone, Mac and Apple Watch only.

Every Lookout claim below comes from Lookout's US App Store listing (version 5.9, released 16 May 2026) and KIDA Studios' Lookout pages on kidastudios.com.

What are the main differences?

  • Separate files. ContentCam's Front & Back mode saves the selfie and the scene as two separate files. Lookout's listing describes "a single, perfectly synced take", the Lookout page on kidastudios.com says an interview gives you "question and answer in one file", and neither mentions separate front and back files.
  • Vertical and horizontal at once. ContentCam saves a 9:16 file and a 16:9 file from one take. Neither Lookout's listing nor kidastudios.com describes that.
  • Android. ContentCam is on iPhone and Android. Lookout is on iPhone, Mac and Apple Watch, and a Google Play search on 6 November 2026 found no Lookout from KIDA Studios.
  • Published frame rate. ContentCam's Front & Back records HD at up to 60fps on iPhones that support it (Premium). Lookout's listing says "Dual-Stream HD/UHD" with no frame rate, and KIDA Studios' own comparison table lists Lookout's multicam resolution as "Not published".
  • Manual controls. ContentCam has manual exposure, white balance, ISO and shutter. Lookout's listing describes voice commands for recording, zoom and snapshots, and does not mention manual ISO or shutter.

Where does ContentCam pull ahead?

Editing later. With the selfie file and the scene file apart, you can cut between full-screen face and full-screen scene in CapCut or DaVinci Resolve, grade each one separately, or run ContentCam's Combine editor for a picture-in-picture or split-screen version and keep the two originals. Trying Combine layouts is free; saving a Combine video is Premium.

One take for every platform. ContentCam's main mode records a 9:16 file for TikTok, Reels and Shorts and a 16:9 file for YouTube at the same time, in HD or 4K, at 24, 25, 30, 50 or 60fps. How that works on a shoot is in filming UGC vertical and horizontal at once.

Android. ContentCam's Front & Back runs on Android phones whose camera system can drive the front and rear cameras together, at up to 30fps in HD; the app checks the phone and only offers the mode where it works. Steps are in recording front and back on Android.

More camera modes. On iPhone, ContentCam adds Dual Lens (wide and ultra-wide at once, iPhone 11 and later, not iPhone SE or 16e), a Front/Back photo that saves the selfie and the rear photo as two separate photos, a teleprompter and timelapse. ContentCam has no ads and no watermark, free or Premium, and Premium comes as yearly (7-day free trial on iPhone), monthly or a one-time lifetime purchase.

What is Lookout (KIDA Studios) built for?

Lookout is built as a capture tool for the Apple ecosystem; KIDA Studios describes it as "Records, does not edit." Lookout runs on iOS 17 or later, has a Mac version (macOS 15.6 or later) that records the Mac webcam and an iPhone via Continuity Camera at the same time from the menu bar, and an Apple Watch companion (watchOS 11.5 or later) with a live preview of both feeds and remote start. Lookout also offers "Hey Lookout" voice commands, a Focus mode that keeps recording with the screen dark, and a route map video (added in version 5.0.1). Its App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected".

What else Lookout lists. Lookout can also record on a Mac (webcam and iPhone together) and start a take from an Apple Watch; ContentCam runs on iPhone and Android phones. Lookout's only listed in-app purchase, Lookout Pro, is US$2.99, and its "Hey Lookout" voice commands let you start recording without touching the phone.

FAQ

Is Lookout Dual Camera Recorder free?

Lookout is free to download. The US App Store lists one in-app purchase, Lookout Pro, at $2.99 (checked 6 November 2026). ContentCam is free to download, has no ads and no watermark on any plan, and gives 5 free Front & Back recordings before Premium.

Is Lookout by KIDA Studios on Android?

No. KIDA Studios lists Lookout for iPhone, Mac and Apple Watch, and a Google Play search on 6 November 2026 returned no Lookout dual camera app from KIDA Studios. ContentCam is on iPhone and Android, and on Android it only offers Front & Back on phones that can run both cameras at once.

Does Lookout save the front and back camera as separate files?

Lookout's App Store listing and kidastudios.com do not describe separate front and back files; the Lookout page on kidastudios.com says an interview gives you "question and answer in one file". ContentCam's Front & Back mode saves the selfie camera and the rear camera as two separate files, and its Combine editor can render a picture-in-picture or split-screen video afterwards, keeping the originals.

What frame rate does Lookout record front and back at?

Lookout's listing says "Dual-Stream HD/UHD" but states no frame rate, and KIDA Studios' own Lookout vs DoubleTake guide lists Lookout's multicam resolution as "Not published". ContentCam's Front & Back records HD at up to 60fps on iPhones that support it (Premium) and up to 30fps on Android.

Can Lookout record vertical and horizontal video at the same time?

Neither Lookout's App Store listing nor kidastudios.com describes saving a 9:16 and a 16:9 file from one take. ContentCam records a vertical 9:16 file and a horizontal 16:9 file at the same time from one lens, in HD or 4K.

Which iPhones can record front and back in Lookout and ContentCam?

KIDA Studios says Lookout's dual capture needs iOS 17 or later and an A12 chip or newer, so iPhone XS, XS Max, XR and later. ContentCam needs iOS 18 or later and records front and back on every iPhone that runs iOS 18, including iPhone 15 and iPhone 16, which do not have Apple's Dual Capture.
