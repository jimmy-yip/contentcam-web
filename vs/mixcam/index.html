ContentCam vs MixCam (2026)

Checked 6 November 2026 · Disclosure: I'm Jimmy and I build ContentCam

Pick ContentCam over MixCam if you want the front and back camera as two separate files you can edit, a vertical and a horizontal video from the same take, and no watermark on any plan. MixCam's listings describe split-screen and picture-in-picture recording, and its free videos carry a watermark that a paid add-on or a Pro plan removes.

Every MixCam claim below comes from MixCam's App Store and Google Play listings and the developer's replies to reviews.

What are the main differences?

  • Separate files. ContentCam's Front & Back mode saves the selfie and the scene as two separate files. MixCam's Google Play listing describes split-screen and picture-in-picture layouts, and neither MixCam listing mentions separate files.
  • Watermark. ContentCam has none, free or Premium. MixCam sells "Remove Watermark" as a separate purchase on the App Store, and on Google Play the developer told a reviewer (9 May 2026) to "upgrade to the PRO version for no watermark".
  • Recording length. On Android, MixCam's developer wrote (14 December 2025): "Record 1 minute (free), or up to 3 minutes (PRO)" with dual cameras.
  • Vertical and horizontal at once. ContentCam saves a 9:16 file and a 16:9 file from one take. Neither MixCam listing describes that.
  • Frame rate and manual controls. ContentCam's Front & Back records up to 60fps in HD on supported iPhones (Premium), with manual ISO, shutter, white balance and exposure in the app. MixCam's listings state no front and back frame rate and no manual ISO or shutter.

Where does ContentCam pull ahead?

Editing later. With the selfie file and the scene file apart, you can cut between full-screen face and full-screen scene in CapCut or DaVinci Resolve, grade each one separately, or run ContentCam's Combine editor for a picture-in-picture or split-screen version and keep the originals. A video with the layout baked in has already made those choices.

One take for every platform. ContentCam's main mode records a 9:16 file for TikTok, Reels and Shorts and a 16:9 file for YouTube at the same time, in HD or 4K, at 24, 25, 30, 50 or 60fps.

How you pay. MixCam's US App Store purchases are a weekly plan, a Business monthly plan, Pro Camera and a Remove Watermark add-on. ContentCam Premium is yearly (7-day free trial on iPhone), monthly, or a one-time lifetime purchase, and all three include the same features. More apps sold once are in dual camera apps with a one-time purchase.

What MixCam reviewers report. Apple's public US review feed holds 139 MixCam reviews (September 2019 to October 2026). Of those, 9 rated 3 stars or lower report missing, faint or "under water" audio, the latest on version 1.38: "picked up no audio" (1 star, 23 November 2024). Another 1-star review objects to paying "weekly just to record a video that is longer than 3 minutes" (22 June 2023).

What is MixCam built for?

MixCam is built for a quick finished dual-camera clip. MixCam has been on the App Store since September 2019, holds 4.7 stars from about 6,000 US ratings, still runs on iOS 15 (iPhone XS, XR and later), and adds a Group Photo mode, full-screen camera switching mid-take, Action Mode and Center Stage support. On Android phones that cannot run two cameras, MixCam offers a dual-camera effect using your own video or photo.

Does MixCam record 4K with both cameras?

MixCam's iPhone listing names no resolution. Its Google Play listing says "Save to your device in 4K, 1080p, or 720p", which describes the saved file rather than what each camera captures (both listings checked 11 November 2026). Apple only lets third-party apps record two cameras in formats it marks as multi-camera capable. On an iPhone 18 Pro that list includes 3840 x 2160 at 30 fps but no 4K format at 60 fps, so 4K at 60 fps can't come from two cameras recording at once. ContentCam's Front & Back records HD, as two separate files. FAQ

Is MixCam free?

MixCam is free to download with in-app purchases. The US App Store lists a weekly plan, a Business monthly plan, Pro Camera and a Remove Watermark add-on (checked 6 November 2026). ContentCam is free to download, has no ads and no watermark on any plan, and gives 5 free Front & Back recordings before Premium.

Does MixCam have a recording time limit?

On Android, yes. MixCam's developer replied to a Google Play review on 14 December 2025: "Record 1 minute (free), or up to 3 minutes (PRO)" with dual cameras, citing battery and heat. The App Store listing does not state a limit for iPhone.

Does MixCam save the front and back camera as separate files?

Neither MixCam listing mentions separate files; the Google Play listing describes split-screen and picture-in-picture layouts. ContentCam's Front & Back mode saves the selfie camera and the rear camera as two separate files, and its Combine editor can render a picture-in-picture or split-screen video afterwards, keeping the originals.

Is MixCam on Android?

Yes. MixCam Dual Camera Front Back is on Google Play (50K+ downloads, 3.9 stars, updated 30 September 2026). Its listing says true dual camera recording needs a phone whose manufacturer supports it. ContentCam is also on Android and only offers Front & Back on phones that can run both cameras at once.

Can MixCam record vertical and horizontal video at the same time?

Neither MixCam listing describes saving a 9:16 and a 16:9 file from one take. ContentCam records a vertical 9:16 file and a horizontal 16:9 file at the same time from one lens, in HD or 4K.
