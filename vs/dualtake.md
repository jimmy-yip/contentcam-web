## Does DualTake record 4K with both cameras?

No, because DualTake doesn't record two cameras at once. Its listing says "Up to 4K, at 24, 30 or 60 fps" and "DualTake records with one camera" (App Store listing, checked 11 November 2026), so its 4K is a single-camera figure, and both of its files are cut from that one frame. Apple only lets third-party apps record two cameras in formats it marks as multi-camera capable. On an iPhone 18 Pro that list includes 3840 x 2160 at 30 fps but no 4K format at 60 fps, so 4K at 60 fps can't come from two cameras recording at once. ContentCam's Single Lens records up to 4K from one camera, and its Front & Back records HD, as two separate files.
