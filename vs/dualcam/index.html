ContentCam vs DualCam (2026)

Checked 10 November 2026 · Disclosure: I'm Jimmy and I build ContentCam

ContentCam and DualCam both save a 9:16 and a 16:9 file from one take and both record the front and back cameras together, but ContentCam does more with them. It runs on iPhone and Android, saves the front and back cameras as two separate files you can edit, has a picture-in-picture window you can drag anywhere, resize and recentre, adds manual exposure, ISO, shutter and white balance plus a built-in teleprompter, and never puts a watermark on your video on any plan. DualCam is iPhone only, and its listing doesn't say whether free videos carry a watermark.

Every DualCam claim below comes from DualCam's App Store listing and its website, dualcam.app.

What are the main differences?

  • Platforms. ContentCam is on iPhone and Android. DualCam is on the App Store and requires iOS 18; I found no Android version from its developer, Mobixoft.
  • Separate files. ContentCam's Front & Back mode saves the selfie and the scene as two separate files. DualCam's listing describes one video with picture-in-picture or split screen, and does not mention separate front and back files.
  • Watermark. ContentCam has none, free or Premium. DualCam's listing doesn't say either way.
  • Manual controls and teleprompter. ContentCam has manual ISO, shutter, white balance and exposure, and a teleprompter. Neither DualCam's listing nor its website mentions a teleprompter or manual ISO, shutter or white balance.
  • Vertical and horizontal at once. Both. DualCam's listing says each dual-format recording saves a Portrait 9:16 file and a Landscape 16:9 file to Photos with identical timestamps, frame for frame.

Where does ContentCam pull ahead?

Android. If your phone is an Android, or you work with someone who shoots on one, ContentCam runs on both. The DualCam listing is for iPhone, and I found no Android version.

Editing later. With the selfie file and the scene file apart, you can cut between full-screen face and full-screen scene in CapCut or DaVinci Resolve, grade each one separately, or run ContentCam's Combine editor for a picture-in-picture or split-screen version and keep the originals. A video with the layout baked in has already made those choices.

Picture-in-picture you control. In ContentCam you can drag the picture-in-picture window anywhere, resize it by dragging, pinching or tapping, and double-tap it to recentre. DualCam's website says its window can be dragged anywhere; it doesn't describe resizing.

No watermark, stated. ContentCam adds no watermark and no ads on any plan, and gives 5 free Front & Back recordings before Premium. DualCam's listing doesn't say whether it adds a watermark, so check the free version before you rely on it.

Camera controls. ContentCam gives you manual exposure, white balance, ISO and shutter, plus a built-in teleprompter, so you can read a script while looking at the lens.

What is DualCam built for?

DualCam is an iPhone app for dual-format and dual-camera clips. Its listing says it records up to 4K and up to 60fps depending on device and mode, in HEVC, as MOV or MP4, with flash control, a crop guide overlay and an estimated file size per minute, and you can switch between dual-camera and single-camera shooting. Its website says you can drag the picture-in-picture window anywhere, swap cameras mid-recording, trim and share in the app, and use it in 30 languages.

On price, the US App Store lists a Lifetime purchase at $24.99, next to Annual at $14.99 and Weekly at $1.99. DualCam has 3.0 stars from 2 US ratings, which is too few to say much either way. It was released on 7 April 2026 and version 1.0.5 was updated on 9 November 2026. The listing says it requires an iPhone XS or later, and that dual-camera recording requires a supported device.

FAQ

Is DualCam free?

DualCam is free to download with in-app purchases. The US App Store lists Weekly at $1.99, Annual at $14.99 and Lifetime at $24.99 (checked 10 November 2026). ContentCam is free to download, has no ads and no watermark on any plan, and gives 5 free Front & Back recordings before Premium.

Is DualCam on Android?

I found no Android version from DualCam's developer, Mobixoft. The App Store listing is for iPhone and requires iOS 18. ContentCam is on iPhone and Android.

Does DualCam save the front and back camera as separate files?

DualCam's listing describes showing yourself and the scene in one video with picture-in-picture or split screen, and does not mention separate front and back files. ContentCam's Front & Back mode saves the selfie camera and the rear camera as two separate files, and its Combine editor can render a picture-in-picture or split-screen video afterwards, keeping the originals.

The App Store listing doesn't say either way. ContentCam adds no watermark on any plan, free or Premium.
