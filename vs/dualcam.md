## Does DualCam record 4K with both cameras?

DualCam's listing doesn't say whether 4K applies when both cameras record. It says "Up to 4K and up to 60 fps, depending on device and mode", and it also lists "Switch between dual-camera and single-camera shooting" (App Store listing, checked 11 November 2026). Apple only lets third-party apps record two cameras in formats it marks as multi-camera capable. On an iPhone 18 Pro that list includes 3840 x 2160 at 30 fps but no 4K format at 60 fps, so 4K at 60 fps can't come from two cameras recording at once. ContentCam's Front & Back records HD, as two separate files.
