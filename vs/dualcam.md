## What are the main differences?

- **Free version.** DualCam gives you the first 60 seconds of recording free, then the video stops and the paywall opens. ContentCam's free version records unlimited Single Lens videos in portrait and landscape at once, in 1080p, plus 5 Dual Lens and 5 Front & Back recordings.
- **4K and 60 fps.** DualCam's settings say "4K is available in single-camera mode only", and 60 fps didn't work in any mode: with 4K and 60 fps selected, the file it saved was 1920 x 1080 at 29.99 fps. ContentCam records 4K with both cameras on supported iPhones, and up to 60 fps.
- **Vertical and horizontal at once.** Both save a 9:16 and a 16:9 file. DualCam cuts the 16:9 file out of the vertical picture, so the horizontal video has the same width as the vertical one. ContentCam's Single Lens records the camera's full 4:3 frame, so the horizontal keeps the full width and the vertical keeps the full height.
- **Camera controls.** DualCam's only manual control is an exposure slider with no reset. Tap to focus shows a yellow square but didn't focus, and there's no AE/AF lock. ContentCam has manual exposure, white balance, ISO and shutter, tap to focus, AE/AF lock, a zoom dial and a teleprompter.
- **Platforms.** ContentCam is on iPhone and Android. DualCam is iPhone only.

## What did I find when I tested DualCam 1.0.5?

On 12 November 2026 I installed DualCam 1.0.5 from the App Store and tried every mode and setting in the free version until the paywall stopped me. This is what I found.

- **60 seconds free, then pay.** The paywall says "First 60 seconds free". When a take reaches one minute, recording stops and the app asks you to subscribe. Plans are weekly, yearly (US$22.99 a year with a 3-day trial, more than ContentCam's yearly plan, which has a 7-day trial) or lifetime.
- **No 4K with both cameras.** The settings screen says "4K is available in single-camera mode only". Front & back recording is HD.
- **No 60 fps.** You can select 4K and 60 fps, but the file I saved was 1080p at 29.99 fps. In dual-format mode the app shows "60 fps unavailable". I didn't get 60 fps in single-camera, dual-format or front & back mode.
- **The 16:9 file is a crop of the vertical one.** In dual-format mode the preview draws a dashed 16:9 box inside the vertical picture, and that box is what the horizontal file gets. The horizontal video shows no more width than the vertical video, and in my test the preview's box didn't line up exactly with what was recorded.
- **Picture-in-picture can't be resized.** Pinching does nothing and there's no size button.
- **Few camera controls.** Exposure is the only manual control, and there's no reset button, so you drag it back to 0 by hand. Tap to focus shows a yellow square but didn't change focus, holding doesn't lock AE/AF, and there are no zoom buttons or zoom dial on the preview.
- **Stabilisation didn't seem to work.** With Video Stabilization on, my handheld clips still looked unstabilised. A 1-star US review from 8 August says "there's no stabilization".
- **Recordings lost.** When I force-closed the app, went to the home screen or locked the phone mid-take, the recording was lost. ContentCam keeps recording through auto-lock and restores a take after a force-close the next time you open it.
- **File names that don't match.** The vertical and horizontal files from the same take get different random names, and the only clue is "_portrait" or "_landscape" at the end. ContentCam saves into projects, scenes and takes with matching file names.
- **Full photo library access.** DualCam asks for full access to your Photo Library "to save your recordings and show your recent videos", and adds its own DualCam album to Photos. Its privacy policy says it doesn't access or read the existing content in your library. ContentCam only asks to add videos to Photos, so it can't see your library.
- **An account you didn't make.** Settings has "Delete account and all data", but the app never asked me to sign up or mentioned an account. Its privacy policy explains that it creates an anonymous account for each install, and sends crash reports to Sentry, including your IP address and a short log of recent in-app actions. ContentCam has no account.
- **Missing features.** No teleprompter, no Dual Lens mode (two rear lenses at once), no 25 or 50 fps, no timelapse, no Apple Log, no audio level meter, and no projects or take management. ContentCam has all of these.
DualCam's paywall: the free version stops at 60 seconds. 4K and 60 fps selected; the saved file was 1080p at 29.99 fps. Dual-format mode: “60 fps unavailable”. The 16:9 file is the dashed box cut from inside the vertical frame. DualCam asks for full access to your photo library.
