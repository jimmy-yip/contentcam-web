ContentCam vs Dualgram (2026)

Tested 12 November 2026 · Listing checked 6 November 2026

Pick ContentCam over Dualgram if you want a front and back camera app with no ads and no watermark on any plan, one that runs on Android as well as iPhone, records a vertical and a horizontal video from the same take, and gives you manual ISO, shutter and white balance. Dualgram is an iPhone and iPad app whose free version carries ads and a watermark until you buy PRO.

Disclosure: I'm Jimmy, I build ContentCam, and I tested Dualgram 2.5 myself on 12 November 2026.

Tested: Dualgram 2.5 doesn't record 4K with both cameras (4K 60 fps is "not supported" on either camera), drops the front camera to 720p when both run at 60 fps, and needs PRO to save the front and back files separately. ContentCam records both cameras in 4K on supported iPhones and at up to 60fps, as two separate files, with no ads or watermark. Full results and screenshots are below.

What are the main differences between ContentCam and Dualgram?

  • Ads and watermark: ContentCam has neither, free or Premium. The Dualgram FAQ says the free app "includes advertisements, watermarks, and certain locked features such as more layouts".
  • Android: ContentCam runs on iPhone and Android. Dualgram has no Android version.
  • Vertical and horizontal at once: ContentCam saves a 9:16 file and a 16:9 file from one take. Dualgram records one shape per take; "Landscape and portrait" in its listing means you can hold the phone either way.
  • Camera controls: both apps have zoom, flash and stabilisation. ContentCam adds manual exposure, white balance, ISO and shutter, tap to focus and hold for AE/AF lock. In my test Dualgram had none of those, and its zoom is a 1x / 0.5x switch.
  • When you choose the layout: ContentCam picks picture-in-picture or split-screen after recording, in its Combine editor. Dualgram sets the layout while you film ("saved exactly as you framed it", per its 2.0 notes) and also saves the two originals.

What is Dualgram built for?

Dualgram, from Singapore studio Just2us, is an iPhone and iPad app. Its listing describes a finished picture-in-picture or side-by-side clip straight out of the camera, with the two originals saved too: its guide lists up to 4K and 60fps per camera depending on the iPhone model, it runs on iOS 17 or later, it lists a built-in teleprompter, and recent updates added a Lock Screen Quick Record widget and iPhone Duo support.

What happened when I tested Dualgram 2.5?

I installed Dualgram 2.5 (build 1224) from the App Store on 12 November 2026 and tried every setting it has, on the free version and against the PRO prompts. This is what I found.

  • 4K with both cameras: no. 4K at 60 fps shows "Oops... the selected resolution/FPS is not supported." on the back and the front camera. 4K at 30 fps on both cameras shows "Your device may not support this resolution and frame rate."
  • 60 fps with both cameras: the front camera drops to 720p. At 1080p 60 fps on both, the settings sheet warns the device may not support it, and once the cameras run the app shows "Device overloaded! Reducing front camera resolution to 720." The saved video was 1080 x 1920, HEVC, at 58.91 fps.
  • Cinematic stabilisation lags the preview. The app warns "Video preview will lag significantly", and the picture on screen ran about 2 seconds behind. Dualgram's user guide says the lag is in the live preview only, with no "lag (or de-sync) in the recorded video file", but it makes framing a moving shot hard. ContentCam runs cinematic stabilisation with a live preview on the main view and the picture-in-picture.
  • Saving the two camera files separately is PRO. The free version saves the combined video only. "Unlock PRO to save the rear camera video separately", and the same for the front.
  • The free version has ads and a watermark. The watermark is a large "Shot with Dualgram" text that sits right above the picture-in-picture window, where your face is.
  • Layouts and moving the window are PRO. Picking another layout shows "Unlock PRO to use this camera layout." The picture-in-picture window can't be resized: pinching does nothing and there is no size button.
  • The teleprompter doesn't scroll. It is a PRO feature, the text sits still on screen with no speed, size or colour settings, and you scroll it with your finger while you talk.
  • No focus or exposure controls. Tapping the preview doesn't focus, there is no AE/AF lock, and zoom is a 1x / 0.5x switch. Frame rates are 24, 30 or 60 fps, with no 25 or 50.
  • A force-closed take was lost. When I closed the app mid-recording the video wasn't saved, and locking the phone mid-take froze the app. ContentCam keeps recording through auto-lock, and recovers a force-quit take on the next launch.
  • No projects or takes. Videos go straight to Photos as DGC_ files, with nothing to sort them by shoot.

Dualgram's low-star App Store reviews describe the same weak spots. One reviewer lost a 46-minute recording when a notification popped up and "the app completely froze"; others report video that stutters or skips frames, audio that goes out of sync, and finding out after download that the separate front and back files cost extra.

Who Dualgram suits: someone who wants one picture-in-picture clip, framed while filming and posted as is. For 4K or 60 fps from both cameras, a teleprompter that scrolls, or a layout you can change after the shoot, it falls short.

Does Dualgram record 4K with both cameras?

Not in my test. On 12 November 2026, Dualgram 2.5 showed "the selected resolution/FPS is not supported" for 4K at 60 fps on both cameras, and "Your device may not support this resolution and frame rate" for 4K at 30 fps on both. Its App Store listing says "Support up to 4k, 60 fps", and its user guide says the front and back cameras can each be set "up to 4K resolution and 60 FPS", adding: "Depending on the iPhone model, certain configurations would not be supported, so you have to try out to know." (checked 11 November 2026). Apple only lets third-party apps record two cameras in formats it marks as multi-camera capable. On an iPhone 18 Pro that list includes 3840 x 2160 at 30 fps but no 4K format at 60 fps, so 4K at 60 fps can't come from two cameras recording at once. ContentCam's Front & Back records in 4K on supported iPhones (from iOS 2.9), as two separate files. FAQ

Does Dualgram have ads or a watermark?

Yes, on the free version. The Dualgram FAQ says the free app includes advertisements, watermarks and some locked features, and buying PRO removes them; the App Store listing shows "Contains Advertising" (checked 6 November 2026). ContentCam has no ads and no watermark, free or Premium.

Is Dualgram on Android?

No. Dualgram is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, with no Android version (checked 6 November 2026). ContentCam is on iPhone and Android.

How much is Dualgram PRO?

On the US App Store, Dualgram sells PRO as a one-year purchase or a one-time unlock, and sells a separate paid Dualgram Pro app with PRO included (checked 6 November 2026). ContentCam Premium comes as yearly, monthly or a one-time lifetime purchase, priced in your store.

Does Dualgram save the front and back camera as separate files?

With PRO. Can Dualgram record vertical and horizontal video at the same time?

No. In my test Dualgram records one shape per take, portrait or landscape depending on how you hold the phone. ContentCam records a 9:16 vertical file and a 16:9 horizontal file at the same time from one lens, in HD or 4K.
