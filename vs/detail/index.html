ContentCam vs Detail (2026)

Checked 6 November 2026 · Disclosure: I'm Jimmy and I build ContentCam

Pick ContentCam over Detail if you want a camera that runs on Android as well as iPhone, records a vertical and a horizontal file at the same time, never adds a watermark, and can be bought once. Detail is an AI video editor for iPhone, iPad and Mac, with free exports watermarked and Detail Pro sold weekly, monthly or yearly.

Every Detail claim below comes from Detail's App Store listing (version 6.0.6, released 4 November 2026), detail.co and Apple's public review feed.

What are the main differences?

  • Android. ContentCam runs on iPhone and Android. Detail offers Detail for iOS and Detail Studio for Mac, and a Google Play search on 6 November 2026 found no Detail app.
  • Watermark. ContentCam has none, free or Premium. detail.co lists the free Basic plan with "Unlimited watermark video exports"; "Watermark-free exports" are a Detail Pro feature.
  • Paying once. ContentCam Premium is yearly, monthly or a one-time lifetime purchase. The in-app purchases on Detail's US App Store page are all subscriptions: Detail Pro Weekly $4.99, Monthly $11.99, Yearly $49.99 or $69.99.
  • Vertical and horizontal at once. ContentCam records a 9:16 file and a 16:9 file at the same time from one take. Detail records one video and, per its changelog, lets you switch it between vertical, horizontal or square after recording.
  • Camera controls. ContentCam has manual ISO, shutter, white balance and exposure, and Front & Back up to 60fps in HD on supported iPhones (Premium). Detail's listing and site do not describe manual ISO or shutter controls, or a front and back frame rate.

Where does ContentCam pull ahead?

Shooting for every platform in one go. ContentCam's main mode records a 9:16 file for TikTok, Reels and Shorts and a 16:9 file for YouTube at the same time, in HD or 4K, at 24, 25, 30, 50 or 60fps. ContentCam's Front & Back mode saves the selfie and the scene as two separate files, ready for CapCut or DaVinci Resolve, and the Combine editor can render a picture-in-picture or split-screen version with the originals kept.

How you pay. No watermark to pay off, no ads, and a lifetime option. Detail's current US list has no lifetime plan; one 3-star reviewer put it as "good value but most of us are tired of subscription" (30 January 2026). More apps sold once are in dual camera apps with a one-time purchase.

What Detail reviewers report. Apple's public US review feed holds 172 Detail reviews (June 2023 to October 2026), 95 of them 3 stars or lower. Of those 95, 10 report audio or video drifting out of sync, mostly in reaction videos and remote podcasts: "the audio goes out of sync whenever I pause the background video" (1 star, 27 July 2026). At least 6 report a recording lost or deleted.

What is Detail built for?

Detail is built for talking videos that come out edited: AI Auto-Edit cuts silences and makes clips, AI Director is a teleprompter that follows your voice, and the app adds captions in 50+ languages, green screen, remote podcast guests and livestreaming to YouTube or Twitch. Detail runs on iOS 17 (iPhone XS, XR and later), iPad and Mac, holds 4.5 stars from about 4,000 US ratings, and detail.co names it the 2025 App Store Award iPad App of the Year. If you want the app to do the edit, Detail is made for that.

FAQ

Is Detail free?

Detail has a free Basic plan, but detail.co lists Basic exports as "Unlimited watermark video exports"; watermark-free exports need Detail Pro. The US App Store lists Detail Pro at $4.99 a week, $11.99 a month, or $49.99 or $69.99 a year (checked 6 November 2026). ContentCam has no watermark and no ads on any plan, and gives 5 free Front & Back recordings before Premium.

Is Detail on Android?

No. detail.co offers Detail for iOS and Detail Studio for Mac only, and a Google Play search on 6 November 2026 found no Detail app from Detail Technologies. ContentCam is on iPhone and Android.

Can Detail record the front and back camera at the same time?

Yes. Detail's own guide records both cameras into a picture-in-picture or grid layout that can be changed after recording, and detail.co recommends iPhone 11 or later for dual camera. ContentCam's Front & Back mode saves the selfie and the rear camera as two separate files, at up to 60fps in HD on iPhones that support it (Premium).

Does Detail have a lifetime purchase?

Not in the current US App Store list, which shows weekly, monthly and yearly Detail Pro subscriptions plus older Legacy plans (checked 6 November 2026). ContentCam Premium comes as a yearly or monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, all with the same features.

Can Detail record vertical and horizontal video at the same time?

Not as two files at once. Detail's changelog says you can switch a recording between vertical, horizontal or square after recording, and suggests shooting with the phone horizontal if you want both. ContentCam records a vertical 9:16 file and a horizontal 16:9 file at the same time from one lens, in HD or 4K.
