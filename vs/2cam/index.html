ContentCam vs 2Cam Dual Shot Camera Recorder (2026)

Checked 6 November 2026 · Disclosure: I'm Jimmy and I build ContentCam

Pick ContentCam over 2Cam if you want the front and back camera as two separate files you can edit, a vertical and a horizontal video from the same take, no watermark on any plan, and the option to pay once instead of weekly. 2Cam's listing describes a picture-in-picture recorder whose watermark is removed by a $5.99 purchase, with weekly, monthly and annual plans.

Every 2Cam claim below comes from 2Cam's App Store listing (version 1.5.2, released 4 November 2026), its Google Play listing and Apple's public US review feed.

What are the main differences?

  • Separate files. ContentCam's Front & Back mode saves the selfie and the scene as two separate files. 2Cam's App Store listing lists "different picture-in-picture shapes" and "Adjust the camera overlay directly on the preview", and neither 2Cam listing mentions separate files.
  • Watermark. ContentCam has none, free or Premium. 2Cam sells "Remove watermark from video" for $5.99 on the US App Store.
  • Lifetime option. ContentCam Premium can be bought once. 2Cam's seven US in-app purchases are weekly ($5.99 and $7.99), monthly ($9.99) and annual ($39.99) plans plus the watermark removal; none is a lifetime plan.
  • Vertical and horizontal at once. ContentCam saves a 9:16 file and a 16:9 file from one take. 2Cam's listing says "Record in portrait or landscape mode", and neither 2Cam listing describes both from one take.
  • Manual controls. ContentCam has manual ISO, shutter, white balance and exposure. 2Cam's listing describes stabilisation, a grid, front-camera mirroring and audio quality settings, and no manual ISO or shutter.

Where does ContentCam pull ahead?

Editing later. With the selfie file and the scene file apart, you can cut between full-screen face and full-screen scene in CapCut or DaVinci Resolve, grade each one separately, or run ContentCam's Combine editor for a picture-in-picture or split-screen version and keep the originals. A video with the inset baked in has already made those choices.

One take for every platform. ContentCam's main mode records a 9:16 file for TikTok, Reels and Shorts and a 16:9 file for YouTube at the same time, in HD or 4K, at 24, 25, 30, 50 or 60fps.

How you pay. ContentCam Premium is yearly (7-day free trial on iPhone), monthly, or a one-time lifetime purchase, and all three include the same features. Other apps sold once are in dual camera apps with a one-time purchase.

What 2Cam reviewers report. Apple's public US review feed holds 14 written 2Cam reviews (March 2024 to October 2026). Of those, 4 one-star reviews object to paying before recording, such as "Can't take video without paying" (1 star, 16 November 2025) and "Subscription for this basic service?" (1 star, 8 July 2025). ContentCam's free tier records vertical and horizontal with no limit on takes, and gives 5 free Front & Back recordings before Premium.

What is 2Cam built for?

2Cam is built for a finished picture-in-picture clip with a script in front of you. It adds a built-in teleprompter with speed, font size and colour settings, several picture-in-picture shapes, an in-app gallery, volume-button recording, 33 languages and support back to iOS 15.2. 2Cam has been on the App Store since December 2022 and holds 4.6 stars from 924 US ratings.

FAQ

Is 2Cam free?

2Cam is free to download with in-app purchases. The US App Store lists weekly plans at $5.99 and $7.99, monthly at $9.99, annual at $39.99 and Remove watermark from video at $5.99 (checked 6 November 2026). ContentCam is free to download, has no ads and no watermark on any plan, and gives 5 free Front & Back recordings before Premium.

Does 2Cam save the front and back camera as separate files?

2Cam's App Store listing describes picture-in-picture shapes and an overlay you adjust on the preview, and neither 2Cam listing mentions separate files. ContentCam's Front & Back mode saves the selfie camera and the rear camera as two separate files, and its Combine editor can render a picture-in-picture or split-screen video afterwards, keeping the originals.

Does 2Cam have a lifetime purchase?

None of the seven in-app purchases on 2Cam's US App Store listing is a lifetime plan; they are weekly, monthly and annual subscriptions plus a $5.99 watermark removal (checked 6 November 2026). ContentCam Premium can be bought once as a lifetime purchase, or as a yearly or monthly subscription, and all three include the same features.

Is 2Cam on Android?

Yes. 2Cam Dual Camera Double Video is on Google Play from the same developer, Vision FZ LLE, with 1K+ downloads and an update on 28 October 2026. ContentCam is also on Android and only offers Front & Back on phones that can run both cameras at once.

Can 2Cam record vertical and horizontal video at the same time?

2Cam's listing says "Record in portrait or landscape mode", and neither 2Cam listing describes saving a 9:16 and a 16:9 file from one take. ContentCam records a vertical 9:16 file and a horizontal 16:9 file at the same time from one lens, in HD or 4K.
