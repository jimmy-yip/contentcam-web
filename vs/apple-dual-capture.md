## What are the main differences between ContentCam and Apple Dual Capture?

- **Which iPhones:** ContentCam's Front & Back runs on every iPhone with iOS 18, including iPhone 15 and iPhone 16. Apple lists Dual Capture on iPhone 17, 17 Pro, 17 Pro Max, Air, 18 Pro, 18 Pro Max and iPhone Duo only.
- **Separate files:** ContentCam saves the selfie and the scene as two files. Apple's guide shows one frame with the front camera as a window inside it.
- **Frame rate:** ContentCam's Front & Back records in HD at up to 60fps on supported iPhones, and in 4K on supported iPhones from iOS 2.9. Apple lists Dual Capture at "up to 4K Dolby Vision at 30 fps".
- **Vertical and horizontal from one take:** ContentCam's main mode saves a 9:16 and a 16:9 file from one recording. Apple's Dual Capture steps describe no such option.
- **Android:** ContentCam is on Android too. Dual Capture is iPhone only.

## FAQ

### Which iPhones have Apple Dual Capture?

Seven, per Apple's iOS 27 iPhone User Guide page "Models that support Dual Capture video": iPhone 17, iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone Air, iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max and iPhone Duo (checked 6 November 2026). The iPhone 16 and iPhone 17e specs pages do not mention Dual Capture. ContentCam's Front & Back mode works on every iPhone that runs iOS 18.

### Can I record front and back at the same time on iPhone 15 or iPhone 16?

Not in Apple's Camera app, because iPhone 15 and iPhone 16 are not on Apple's Dual Capture list. ContentCam records the front and back camera at the same time on iPhone 15 and iPhone 16, and on any other iPhone running iOS 18, saving two separate files.

### Does Apple Dual Capture save the front and back camera as separate files?

Apple's guide shows one frame, with "a window in the top-left of the frame" for the front camera and the rest of the frame for the back camera, and documents no option for separate files. ContentCam's Front & Back saves the selfie camera and the rear camera as two separate files; its Combine editor can make a picture-in-picture or split-screen video from them afterwards, with the originals kept.

### What frame rate does Apple Dual Capture record at?

Apple's specs pages list "Dual Capture up to 4K Dolby Vision at 30 fps" for iPhone 17, iPhone Air, iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Duo. ContentCam's Front & Back records in 4K on supported iPhones (from iOS 2.9), and in HD at up to 60fps on iPhones that support it (60fps is a Premium option; some older iPhones cap at 30fps).

### Is Apple Dual Capture on Android?

No. Dual Capture is a mode in Apple's Camera app on iPhone. ContentCam records front and back on Android phones whose camera system can run the front and rear cameras at once, in HD at up to 30fps; the app checks and only offers the mode where it works.
