ContentCam vs Apple Dual Capture (iPhone 17 and later, 2026)

Checked 6 November 2026

Pick ContentCam over Apple Dual Capture if your iPhone is an iPhone 15, iPhone 16 or any other model without Dual Capture, if you want the front and back camera as two separate files you can edit, if you want 60fps, or if you are on Android. Apple Dual Capture is a free mode in the Camera app on seven iPhone models that records both cameras into one frame, with the selfie in a window in the top-left corner.

Disclosure: I'm Jimmy and I build ContentCam. Every Apple claim below comes from Apple's iOS 27 iPhone User Guide, the iPhone specs pages and Apple's September 2025 iPhone 17 announcement, checked 6 November 2026.

What are the main differences between ContentCam and Apple Dual Capture?

  • Which iPhones: ContentCam's Front & Back runs on every iPhone with iOS 18, including iPhone 15 and iPhone 16. Apple lists Dual Capture on iPhone 17, 17 Pro, 17 Pro Max, Air, 18 Pro, 18 Pro Max and iPhone Duo only.
  • Separate files: ContentCam saves the selfie and the scene as two files. Apple's guide shows one frame with the front camera as a window inside it.
  • Frame rate: ContentCam's Front & Back records HD at up to 60fps on supported iPhones. Apple lists Dual Capture at "up to 4K Dolby Vision at 30 fps".
  • Vertical and horizontal from one take: ContentCam's main mode saves a 9:16 and a 16:9 file from one recording. Apple's Dual Capture steps describe no such option.
  • Android: ContentCam is on Android too. Dual Capture is iPhone only.

Where does ContentCam pull ahead of Apple Dual Capture?

Older iPhones. The iPhone 16 and iPhone 17e specs pages don't mention Dual Capture, and Apple ties the feature to the 18MP Center Stage front camera (Apple's iPhone 17 page says it "enables Dual Capture"), a camera the iPhone 16 specs page does not list. ContentCam records front and back on any iPhone running iOS 18, from iPhone XS and XR on. Steps are in recording front and back on iPhone 15 and 16.

Two files, layout decided later. With the selfie file and the scene file apart, you can cut between full-screen face and full-screen scene in CapCut or DaVinci Resolve, colour-grade each one separately, or drop one entirely. ContentCam's Combine editor turns the pair into a picture-in-picture or split-screen video after the shoot (trying layouts is free; saving a Combine video is Premium), and the originals stay. Apple's guide shows the Dual Capture window inside the frame itself; what that rules out is in the Dual Capture limits post.

60fps with both cameras. On iPhones that support it, ContentCam's Front & Back records both cameras in HD at up to 60fps (Premium; some older iPhones cap at 30fps). Apple's ceiling for Dual Capture is 30fps, on a phone that shoots 4K at 60fps with one camera.

Android. ContentCam's Front & Back works on Android phones whose camera system can run front and rear cameras at once, in HD at up to 30fps; the app only offers the mode where it works. See front and back at the same time on Android.

A creator camera around it. ContentCam adds manual exposure, white balance, ISO and shutter, a teleprompter, timelapse, and 24, 25, 30, 50 or 60fps in HD or 4K in its single-lens modes. No ads and no watermark on any plan.

What is Apple Dual Capture built for?

Apple Dual Capture is built for a finished clip with nothing to install: it is free, already in the Camera app (Video mode, camera controls, Dual Capture), and records up to 4K Dolby Vision at 30fps, with Apple's 18MP Center Stage front camera on the selfie side (Apple's iPhone 17 page says that camera "enables Dual Capture"). If the clip you record is the clip you post and your iPhone is on Apple's list, Dual Capture does that job well.

FAQ

Which iPhones have Apple Dual Capture?

Seven, per Apple's iOS 27 iPhone User Guide page "Models that support Dual Capture video": iPhone 17, iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone Air, iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max and iPhone Duo (checked 6 November 2026). The iPhone 16 and iPhone 17e specs pages do not mention Dual Capture. ContentCam's Front & Back mode works on every iPhone that runs iOS 18.

Can I record front and back at the same time on iPhone 15 or iPhone 16?

Not in Apple's Camera app, because iPhone 15 and iPhone 16 are not on Apple's Dual Capture list. ContentCam records the front and back camera at the same time on iPhone 15 and iPhone 16, and on any other iPhone running iOS 18, saving two separate files.

Does Apple Dual Capture save the front and back camera as separate files?

Apple's guide shows one frame, with "a window in the top-left of the frame" for the front camera and the rest of the frame for the back camera, and documents no option for separate files. ContentCam's Front & Back saves the selfie camera and the rear camera as two separate files; its Combine editor can make a picture-in-picture or split-screen video from them afterwards, with the originals kept.

What frame rate does Apple Dual Capture record at?

Apple's specs pages list "Dual Capture up to 4K Dolby Vision at 30 fps" for iPhone 17, iPhone Air, iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Duo. ContentCam's Front & Back records HD at up to 60fps on iPhones that support it (60fps is a Premium option; some older iPhones cap at 30fps).

Is Apple Dual Capture on Android?

No. Dual Capture is a mode in Apple's Camera app on iPhone. ContentCam records front and back on Android phones whose camera system can run the front and rear cameras at once, in HD at up to 30fps; the app checks and only offers the mode where it works.
