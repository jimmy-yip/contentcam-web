## In one line

ContentCam records portrait (9:16) and landscape (16:9) video at the same time, so one take gives creators a vertical video for TikTok, Reels and Shorts and a horizontal one for YouTube.

## The story

Jimmy built ContentCam for his girlfriend, who is a creator. He kept watching her film every scene twice: once vertical for Reels and TikTok, once horizontal for YouTube. Cropping one out of the other doesn't fix it, because a horizontal strip cut from a vertical video loses the sides of the scene and most of the resolution.

So ContentCam reads the camera's full 4:3 frame and cuts both videos from it. The landscape file keeps the full width of the scene and the portrait keeps its full height, from a single tap.

> "I kept watching her film every scene twice, and figured one take should give you both." (Jimmy Yip, developer)

> "Tried three other apps before this, none worked as good as this one. Most just crop the same view, but this app you can actually choose between single or dual lens, which gives different outputs." (aimeèy, App Store review, Australia, May 2026)

## What sets it apart

- **Two files, not a crop.** Most apps record vertical and cut a horizontal strip out of the middle. ContentCam gives each format its own full-frame framing.
- **Front & Back on more phones.** Apple's Dual Capture is limited to recent iPhones and saves one picture-in-picture file. ContentCam's Front & Back works on any iPhone running iOS 18 and on supported Android phones, as two separate files.
- **Built by one person.** A solo developer who reads every review and feature request, and ships an update every week or two.
