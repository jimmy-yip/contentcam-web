ContentCam changelog

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Is ContentCam still being updated? Yes. The current versions are iOS 2.8.1 (4 November 2026) and Android 2.7.0 (October 2026), and there have been 11 iPhone releases since May 2026. Every update is listed below, newest first. ContentCam is built by Jimmy, a solo developer in Sydney, who reads every feature request and bug report.

iPhone

iOS 2.8.1 · November 2026

  • Maintenance and polish across recording and the library.

iOS 2.8 · October 2026

  • Combine: turn a Front & Back recording into one video, with picture-in-picture or split screen.
  • PAL formats: record at 25 or 50 fps (turn them on in Settings).
  • Teleprompter scripts can now be up to 15,000 characters.

iOS 2.7 · October 2026

  • Pause and resume while recording: one recording, with the paused part removed.

iOS 2.6.1 · October 2026

  • Telephoto lens (BETA) on iPhones whose telephoto is longer than 2×.
  • Step to the next or previous recording with arrows.
  • Select several recordings and share or save them in one go, trims included.

iOS 2.6 · September 2026

  • Choose the folder each project's recordings go into.
  • Move recordings between scenes and projects, and reorder your library.
  • Now in French, Japanese and Indonesian: eight languages in all.

iOS 2.5 · September 2026

  • Photo mode: one press saves a 9:16 and a 16:9 photo of the same moment.
  • AE/AF lock: press and hold the viewfinder.
  • Fully translated into German.

iOS 2.4.1 · September 2026

  • Audio fixes: fewer dropouts, better sync, and sound that resumes after a call or Siri.

iOS 2.4 · August 2026

  • Front & Back records both cameras at 60fps.
  • Three sizes for the smaller preview.
  • Select and delete several takes at once.
  • Fully translated into Spanish.

iOS 2.3.2 · August 2026

  • Fixes: audio dropouts and drift, 60fps now truly records at 60fps, record button hang, landscape preview recovery.

iOS 2.3 · August 2026

  • Steadier footage in every mode, and true 10-bit HDR recording.
  • The ultra-wide 0.5x lens works on the back camera in Front & Back.
  • Front-camera mirroring setting, start and stop tones, countdown torch flash, front-camera zoom.

iOS 1.0 · May 2026

  • First release on the App Store: vertical and horizontal video at the same time, from one take.

Android

Android 2.7.0 · October 2026

  • Combine Front & Back recordings into one video, as picture-in-picture or split screen.
  • 25 and 50 fps on phones that offer them.
  • Arrows to step through takes; manual controls stay as you set them; rebuilt background blur.

Android 2.6.0 · October 2026

  • Pause and resume while recording.
  • Improved focus; share several takes at once from Projects.

Android 2.5.1 · October 2026

  • Clearer video on Google Pixel phones, especially on the 0.5x ultra-wide.

Android 2.5.0 · October 2026

  • Background blur (Beta).
  • Choose the folder recordings go into; move and reorder your library.
  • Teleprompter turns for landscape filming; French, Japanese and Indonesian.

Android 2.4.0 · September 2026

  • Photo mode: a vertical and a horizontal photo of the same moment.
  • AE/AF lock, telephoto and 25fps support; German.

Android 2.3.0 · August 2026

  • Italian, Portuguese and Spanish.
  • Stabilisation on by default.
