Isn't one just a crop of the other?

No. Your landscape keeps the whole scene.

Most apps record vertical, then cut a horizontal strip out of the middle for YouTube. You lose the sides of the scene and most of the resolution. ContentCam uses the camera's full frame for both, so each video gets its own framing, and the landscape shows the whole width of the scene at full resolution.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Isn't the landscape just a crop of the portrait?

No. In Single Lens mode ContentCam records the sensor's full 4:3 frame and cuts each format from it, so the landscape keeps the full width and the portrait keeps the full height. Each file is encoded on its own in HEVC, at 1080×1920 and 1920×1080 or the 4K equivalents. Neither is an upscaled crop of the other.

Is ContentCam free, or do I need a subscription?

It's free to download and use: Single Lens recording in both formats, 1080p, 30fps, three projects and no watermark.

Premium is optional, and you choose how to pay: an annual plan with a free trial, a monthly plan, or a one-time lifetime purchase. They all unlock the same features, priced in your local currency in the App Store and Google Play.

Can I try the premium features before paying?

Yes. Every premium feature can be previewed live, including the manual dials. The paywall only appears when you press record with a premium feature switched on.

Dual Lens and Front & Back also give you 5 free recordings each, and the annual plan starts with a free trial.

Are there ads or a watermark?

No. No ads and no watermark, on any tier, free included.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone XS and later on iOS 18 or newer; Dual Lens mode needs iPhone 11 or later with an ultra-wide camera (not iPhone SE or iPhone 16e).

Android 8.0 or newer; 4K, 60fps and manual exposure depend on what your phone's camera supports. Dual Lens and HDR are iPhone-only for now.

Can it record the front and back cameras at the same time?

Yes, on iPhone and Android, as two separate files or one combined video. Front & Back works on every iPhone on iOS 18, including iPhone 15 and 16, and is a premium mode with 5 free recordings to try.

Recent iPhones such as iPhone 17 also have Apple's built-in Dual Capture, which saves both cameras as one picture-in-picture file: which iPhones have Dual Capture. How to do it on Android →

How is it different from DualShot Recorder?

Both record portrait and landscape from one take. DualShot Recorder is iPhone-only; ContentCam runs on iPhone and Android, adds Dual Lens (wide and ultra-wide at once on iPhone 11 and later) and a projects, scenes and takes workflow with automatic file naming, and still offers a one-off lifetime purchase alongside its subscriptions.
