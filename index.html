FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Isn't the landscape just a crop of the portrait?

No. In Single Lens mode ContentCam records the sensor's full 4:3 frame and cuts each format from it, so the landscape keeps the full width and the portrait keeps the full height. Each file is encoded on its own in HEVC, at 1080×1920 and 1920×1080 or the 4K equivalents. Neither is an upscaled crop of the other.

Is ContentCam free, or do I need a subscription?

It's free to download and use: Single Lens recording in both formats, 1080p, 30fps, three projects and no watermark. Premium is optional and sold three ways: an annual plan with a free trial, a monthly plan, or a one-off lifetime purchase. All three unlock the same features, priced in your own currency in the App Store and Google Play.

Can I try the premium features before paying?

Yes. Every premium feature can be previewed live, including the manual dials. The paywall only appears when you press record with a premium feature switched on. Dual Lens and Front & Back also give you 5 free recordings each, and the annual plan starts with a free trial.

Is there a watermark?

Never, on any tier, free included.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone XS and later on iOS 18 or newer; Dual Lens mode needs iPhone 11 or later. Android 8.0 or newer; 4K, 60fps and manual exposure depend on what your phone's camera supports. Dual Lens and HDR are iPhone-only for now.

Can it record the front and back cameras at the same time?

Yes, on iPhone and Android. Front & Back is a premium mode with 5 free recordings to try. How to do it on Android →

How is it different from DualShot Recorder?

Both record portrait and landscape from one take. DualShot Recorder is iPhone-only; ContentCam runs on iPhone and Android, adds Dual Lens (wide and ultra-wide at once on iPhone 11 and later) and a projects, scenes and takes workflow with automatic file naming, and still offers a one-off lifetime purchase alongside its subscriptions.
