How one take becomes two videos

Most "dual format" apps record portrait, then chop the top and bottom off to fake a landscape file. You lose everything left and right of the phone frame. ContentCam works differently.

Single Lens mode

Records from the sensor's native 4:3 frame, then crops 9:16 and 16:9 out of it. The landscape file keeps the full horizontal field of view, and the portrait keeps the full vertical. Neither is a crop of the other.

Dual Lens mode

On iPhone 11 and later, the wide and ultra-wide cameras record at the same time. Portrait from one lens, landscape from the other, with genuinely different framing. iOS only for now.

Front camera too

Talking-head creators get the same two files from the selfie camera.

Both files, instantly

Stop recording and both versions are ready to review side by side. Save, share, or delete each one.

Quick answers

Does recording both formats halve the quality?

No. Both files are encoded independently in HEVC: portrait at 1080×1920 and landscape at 1920×1080 (or 4K equivalents).

Which phones does it support?

iPhone XS and later on iOS 18+. Dual Lens mode needs iPhone 11 or later. On Android, any phone with a reasonable camera; features like 4K and manual exposure depend on the hardware.

Is there a watermark?

No watermarks on any tier, free included.
