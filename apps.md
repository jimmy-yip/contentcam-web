## Shoot

### Blackmagic Camera

Turns your phone into a cinema camera, with full manual controls so it stops auto-adjusting mid-shot. [Get](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6449580241)

### Kino

Pick a colour preset made by real filmmakers and your video comes out already graded. No editing. [Get](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6472380172)

### Dazz Cam

Film in 8mm, 16mm or VHS for that vintage look, straight out of the camera. [Get](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1422471180)

### Final Cut Camera

Apple's own pro camera app: full manual controls, ProRes and Log on Pro iPhones. [Get](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6469552837)

## Edit

### Edits by Instagram

Writes your captions for you, with green screen, cutouts and overlays built in. [Get](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6738967378)

### Adobe Premiere

Filmed somewhere noisy? Enhance Speech cleans up your voice so it sounds like you used a mic. [Get](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6742757464)

## Post

### TikTok Studio

TikTok's own app for creators: deeper analytics, post ideas and monetisation programs in one place. [Get](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6478151677)
