Two formats, one take

Portrait and landscape from the same moment

Single Lens mode uses the camera's full 4:3 frame and cuts both shapes from it. The portrait keeps the full height, the horizontal video keeps the full width, and each one is saved as its own file.

  • A vertical video for TikTok, Reels and Shorts and a horizontal video for YouTube, every time you press record Free
  • 1080p in both formats, as many recordings as you like Free
  • 4K, set separately for each format Premium
  • Portrait only when you don't need the horizontal video Free

Recording modes

More than one way to film

Switch modes from the camera screen. The two big Premium modes come with free recordings, so you can see what you get before paying.

  • Dual Lens: the wide and ultra-wide cameras at once, so the two videos have different framing. iPhone 11 or later. Premium · 5 free
  • Front & Back: your face and the scene in one take. Keep them as separate files, or Combine them into one video, picture-in-picture or split screen. Premium · 5 free
  • Front camera in both formats Free
  • Photo mode: a 9:16 and a 16:9 photo in one tap Premium
  • Timelapse in both formats Premium

Camera controls

Leave it on auto, or take over

Every Premium dial works in the live preview first. You only see the paywall when you press record with one switched on.

  • Tap to focus, hold for AE/AF lock Free
  • Exposure compensation Free
  • Manual white balance, ISO and shutter speed Premium
  • 24, 25 or 30fps Free
    50 or 60fps Premium
  • Stabilisation on by default, plus HDR video on iPhone Free
  • Background blur, in beta on Android Premium
  • Grid, level and audio meter Free
