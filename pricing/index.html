Pricing questions

Do all three plans unlock the same features?

Yes. Annual, monthly and lifetime unlock the identical premium feature set. The only difference is how you pay.

How does the free trial work?

The trial is on the annual plan. You get full premium for the trial period; cancel before it ends and you pay nothing. Apple and Google both remind you before a trial converts.

What happens if I cancel my subscription?

You keep premium until the end of the period you paid for, then the app drops back to the free tier. Your recordings and projects are never touched.

Is the lifetime purchase really one-off?

Yes. One payment, no renewal, and it includes future premium features. On iOS it supports Family Sharing.

Where do I see the actual price?

In the app, or on the App Store and Google Play listings. Pricing is set per region, so quoting a single number here would be wrong for most of the world. The store always shows the current price in your own currency before you commit to anything.

Why is monthly more per month than annual?

Honest answer: monthly exists for people who want premium for one project. If you'll use ContentCam beyond a month or two, annual or lifetime is the sensible buy.
