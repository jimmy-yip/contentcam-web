Pricing questions

Do all three plans unlock the same features?

Yes. Annual, monthly and lifetime unlock the identical premium feature set. The only difference is how you pay.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

The trial is on the annual plan. You get full premium for 7 days; cancel before it ends and you pay nothing. Apple and Google both remind you before a trial converts.

What happens if I cancel my subscription?

You keep premium until the end of the period you paid for, then the app drops back to the free tier. Your recordings and projects are never touched.

Is the lifetime purchase really one-off?

Yes. US$39.99 once, no renewal, includes future premium features. On iOS it supports Family Sharing.

Why is monthly so expensive compared to annual?

Honest answer: monthly exists for people who want premium for one project. If you'll use ContentCam beyond a month or two, annual or lifetime is the sensible buy.
