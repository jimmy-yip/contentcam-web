How to Record the Front and Back Camera at the Same Time on iPhone 15 and 16

By Jimmy, developer of ContentCam · 5 November 2026 · Updated 12 November 2026

To record the front and back camera at the same time on an iPhone 15 or iPhone 16, use a third-party camera app with a front and back mode, because neither phone has Apple's Dual Capture in the Camera app. ContentCam's Front & Back mode, which I build, is my pick for both phones: it runs on iOS 18 or later, saves your face and the scene as two separate files with no ads or watermark, and Premium can combine them into one picture-in-picture or split-screen video.

Disclosure: I build ContentCam, so I'm not neutral. Every claim about another app below comes from that app's App Store listing, its own website or its US App Store reviews, checked 5 November 2026.

Does the iPhone 15 or iPhone 16 have Dual Capture?

No. Apple lists Dual Capture, the Camera app's front and back video mode, for seven phones: iPhone 17, iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone Air, iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max and iPhone Duo. Every iPhone 15 model and every iPhone 16 model, including iPhone 16e, is missing from that list. The details of what Dual Capture does on the newer phones are in which iPhones have Dual Capture and its limits.

Can an iPhone 15 or iPhone 16 record both cameras at once?

Yes. The iPhone 15 and iPhone 16 support Apple's multi-camera capture for apps, so a third-party camera app can run the front camera and the back camera together; only the Camera app lacks the mode. The apps differ in one way that matters: one finished video or two separate files.

FAQ

Does the iPhone 15 have Dual Capture?

No. The iPhone 15 range is not on Apple's Dual Capture list, which starts at iPhone 17, so the Camera app on any iPhone 15 has no front and back mode.

Does the iPhone 16 have Dual Capture?

No. No iPhone 16 model, including iPhone 16e, has Dual Capture in the Camera app. A third-party app such as ContentCam can still record both cameras at once on an iPhone 16.
