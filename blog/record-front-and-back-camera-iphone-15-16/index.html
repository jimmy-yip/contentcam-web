How to Record the Front and Back Camera at the Same Time on iPhone 15 and 16

By Jimmy, developer of ContentCam · 5 November 2026

To record the front and back camera at the same time on an iPhone 15 or iPhone 16, use a third-party camera app with a front and back mode, because neither phone has Apple's Dual Capture in the Camera app. ContentCam's Front & Back mode, which I build, saves your face and the scene as two separate files on both phones, and Premium can combine them into one picture-in-picture video. SplitRig and RØDE Capture are free options that do it too.

Disclosure: I build ContentCam, so I'm not neutral. Every claim about another app below comes from its App Store listing, checked 5 November 2026, and I've said where those apps beat mine.

Does the iPhone 15 or iPhone 16 have Dual Capture?

No. Apple lists Dual Capture, the Camera app's front and back video mode, for seven phones: iPhone 17, iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone Air, iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max and iPhone Duo. Every iPhone 15 model and every iPhone 16 model, including iPhone 16e, is missing from that list. The details of what Dual Capture does on the newer phones are in which iPhones have Dual Capture and its limits.

Can an iPhone 15 or iPhone 16 record both cameras at once?

Yes. The iPhone 15 and iPhone 16 support Apple's multi-camera capture for apps, so a third-party camera app can run the front camera and the back camera together. What you can't do on an iPhone 15 or iPhone 16 is turn it on in the Camera app itself. You need an app, and the apps differ in one way that matters: whether you get one finished video or two separate files.

How do you record front and back on iPhone 15 or 16 in ContentCam?

  1. Install ContentCam from the App Store. It needs iOS 18 or later, which every iPhone 15 and iPhone 16 runs.
  2. Switch the recording mode to Front & Back.
  3. Press record once. The front camera and the back camera start together and save as two separate files.
  4. With Premium, Combine turns the two files into one picture-in-picture or split-screen video in the app.

The separate files are the point for anyone who edits: the face file and the scene file start at the same moment, so you can line them up and cut between them or place the inset yourself in CapCut, Final Cut or DaVinci Resolve.

FAQ

Does the iPhone 15 have Dual Capture?

No. Apple lists Dual Capture for iPhone 17, iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone Air, iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max and iPhone Duo. The iPhone 15 range is not on the list, so the Camera app on an iPhone 15 has no front and back mode.

Does the iPhone 16 have Dual Capture?

No. iPhone 16, iPhone 16 Plus, iPhone 16 Pro, iPhone 16 Pro Max and iPhone 16e do not have Dual Capture in the Camera app. A third-party camera app can still record the front and back camera at the same time on an iPhone 16.

What is the best free app to record front and back on iPhone 15?

SplitRig and RØDE Capture are both free. SplitRig needs iOS 26 and saves one combined video. RØDE Capture runs on iOS 16 and later and, per its App Store listing, can save a combined file or separate files.

Can I get the front and back camera as two separate files on iPhone 16?

Yes. ContentCam's Front & Back mode, RØDE Capture and Dualgram all list separate files. ContentCam saves the front camera and the back camera as two separate video files, and with Premium it can combine them into one picture-in-picture or split-screen video.

What resolution do front and back apps record on iPhone 15 and 16?

ContentCam records HD when both cameras run, because on every iPhone I've tested, Apple's multi-camera formats top out at 1920×1440. Apple's own Dual Capture reaches 4K at 30fps, but only on iPhone 17 and newer models.

On iPhone 17, iPhone Air, iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Duo you also have Apple's own Dual Capture.
