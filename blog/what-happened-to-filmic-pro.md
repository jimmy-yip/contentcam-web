## Is Filmic Pro still being updated in 2026?

The iOS listing shows Filmic Pro at **version 7.5.35, updated 8 September 2026**, sold by **Bending Spoons Operations SpA**. The release notes say only that the team "needed to fix some minor bugs". The Google Play listing shows **version 7.6.4, last updated 5 November 2025**, exactly 12 months before these figures were checked, and it rates **2.3 stars from 17.9K reviews**.

## What actually happened to Filmic Pro?

1. **September 2022: Filmic was acquired by Bending Spoons**. The one-time purchase became a subscription.
2. **December 2023: the entire Filmic staff was laid off.** Reported by [Daring Fireball on 5 December 2023](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2023/12/05/filmic-staff-laid-off) and [CineD](https://www.cined.com/future-of-filmic-pro-mobile-app-unclear-after-mass-layoffs/). Development continued inside Bending Spoons.
3. **Since then: bug fixes on iPhone, silence on Android.**

If you bought Filmic Pro v6 outright before **25 August 2022**, you can still get **Filmic Legacy**, a separate app that its listing says "will continue to receive bug fixes but will not receive any new features". Everyone else pays by subscription.

## How much does Filmic Pro cost in 2026?

It is free to download and sold by subscription, in weekly and yearly lengths. The US App Store in-app purchase list shows **Filmic Weekly at $2.99**, **Creator Weekly at $9.99**, **Filmic Yearly with free trial at $39.99** and a **Cinematographer Kit at $13.99**.

The subscription is the main complaint in its reviews. Of the 50 most recent written reviews on the US App Store (27 March to 3 November 2026), **13 complain about the subscription or paywall**, and 2 say recordings were lost or not saved. One 1-star review on 17 July 2026: "FP gives you a week to try it, then charge you $10/wk". Another, 1 star on 3 June 2026: "Constantly crashing and I lose the whole video."

## What should you use instead of Filmic Pro?

**ContentCam, if you film for TikTok, Reels, Shorts or YouTube.** Filmic Pro's own listing pitches it at "vloggers, and social media content creators", and that is the job ContentCam is built for. It records [9:16 vertical and 16:9 horizontal at the same time](https://contentcam.app/blog/record-portrait-and-landscape-video-same-time-iphone/) from one take, as two separate files. It has manual exposure, white balance, ISO and shutter, 24 to 60fps in HD or 4K, and a built-in teleprompter. The free tier records both formats in HD with no account. It runs on iPhone (iOS 18 or later) and Android.

**Blackmagic Camera, if you grade Log or ProRes.** Free, from Blackmagic Design, it records 10-bit Apple ProRes and works with Apple Log on recent iPhones. ContentCam does not shoot Log or ProRes, so for grading this is the pick.

**Kino** ($9.99) offers preset looks. **Final Cut Camera** (free) does Live Multicam with Final Cut Pro for iPad. **ProCam** is $9.99, with 4K video a separate $4.99 purchase.

**On Android, ContentCam or Blackmagic Camera.** ContentCam runs on Android with the same dual-format recording. [Blackmagic Camera for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.blackmagicdesign.android.blackmagiccam) is free and was updated on 29 October 2026, but its listing warns that "some features are device dependent and may not be available on your device".
