The Dual Camera Effect: How to Film Front and Back at the Same Time

By Jimmy, developer of ContentCam · 15 August 2026 · Updated 5 November 2026

To use the front and back camera at the same time, switch on a dual camera mode before you record. On iPhone 17, iPhone Air, iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Duo that is Dual Capture in the Camera app. On any other iPhone running iOS 18 or later, and on Android phones that can run both cameras, ContentCam's Front & Back mode records your face and the scene as two separate files. Snapchat and Instagram work for a quick in-app clip.

I build ContentCam, so weigh that. Claims about other apps were checked on their makers' own pages on 5 November 2026.

What is the dual camera effect?

It's a video where the front and back cameras record at once: the main shot fills the screen and your face sits in a small inset, or the two are stacked as a split screen. It's the look behind most reaction videos and walking vlogs, and because both share one timeline, the reaction timing is real.

Why record the two cameras as separate files?

Every built-in option bakes the layout into one file at capture. Separate files let you:

  • Place the inset later. Move it, resize it, or hide it when it covers the shot.
  • Cut between angles. Full screen on your face for the reaction, then full screen on the scene.
  • Keep clean B-roll with no face on it.
  • Edit anywhere. Instagram and Snapchat give you one combined clip, not two files for CapCut or Premiere.

How do you do it in ContentCam?

  1. Open ContentCam and switch to Front & Back.
  2. Record once. The rear camera and the selfie camera come out of the same take as two files.
  3. To post one video, open the Combine editor and pick picture-in-picture or split screen. Trying layouts is free; saving a combined video is Premium. The original files are kept.

No ads and no watermark, free or Premium. Front & Back records HD: up to 60 fps on supported iPhones (60 fps is Premium), up to 30 fps on Android.

How do you use Dual Capture on iPhone?

Per Apple's iPhone User Guide: open Camera, select Video, tap the Camera Controls button, tap Dual Capture, then tap the screen and record. Apple lists seven models (in the table above); iPhone 16 and earlier, iPhone 16e and iPhone 17e don't have it. More in iPhone 17 Dual Capture and where it stops.

How do you use both cameras at the same time on Android?

Use a camera app with a front and back mode, or check Instagram's Dual. Apple's Dual Capture is iPhone only, and Snapchat's help centre still says Dual Camera "will be available on Android soon". Hardware decides the rest: Android 11 added getConcurrentCameraIds(), which reports which cameras can run together, and ContentCam only offers Front & Back where it works.

Why is the Instagram dual camera not showing?

Meta announced Dual in July 2022 as a Reels tool to "record using your phone's front and back cameras simultaneously". If it's missing, update Instagram, look in the Reels camera and then Stories, and check the phone can run two cameras at once. Failing that, record with Dual Capture or a front and back camera app (see the dual-camera apps compared) and upload the clip as a Reel.

How do you take a selfie and a back camera photo at the same time?

Snapchat's Dual Camera takes one Snap with both cameras in a fixed layout; Dual Capture and Instagram's Dual are video only. ContentCam's Front & Back photo saves the selfie and the rear shot as two separate photos from one press, on iPhone and Android (a Premium feature). Step by step here.

Can you get the dual camera effect in portrait and landscape?

Not with the built-in modes, which record one aspect ratio; cropping vertical to 16:9 keeps about a third of the frame. ContentCam's core mode records a scene as a vertical file and a wide file at the same time, so you can shoot for TikTok and YouTube without reshooting.

Common questions

Do I need an iPhone 17 to use both cameras at once? No. Dual Capture needs an iPhone 17 model, iPhone Air, iPhone 18 Pro model or iPhone Duo. Is the dual camera effect free? The built-in versions are: Instagram's Dual, Snapchat's Dual Camera and the iPhone's Dual Capture each give you one finished file with the layout fixed. ContentCam's Front & Back gives 5 free recordings as two separate files, then needs Premium.

Can I move the small frame afterwards? Not with the built-in options, which bake the layout into one file. You need the two cameras saved as separate files, which is what ContentCam's Front & Back mode records.
