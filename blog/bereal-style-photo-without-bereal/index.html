How to Take a BeReal-Style Front and Back Photo Without BeReal (2026)

By Jimmy, developer of ContentCam · 6 October 2026 · Updated 5 November 2026

To take a BeReal-style front and back photo without BeReal, use Snapchat's Dual Camera on an iPhone for one combined image, or ContentCam's Front & Back photo on iPhone or Android if you want the selfie and the back photo as two separate photos. The iPhone Camera app's Dual Capture and Instagram's Dual both record video, so they only give you a still if you screenshot a frame.

Disclosure: I build ContentCam, so I'm not neutral. Every claim about BeReal, Snapchat, Instagram and Apple comes from their own help pages, listings or announcements, checked 5 November 2026. The general version of this question is in how to take a selfie and a back camera photo at the same time.

What does BeReal actually save when you take a BeReal?

BeReal saves one post made of a front photo and a back photo. The BeReal App Store listing (version 4.99.0, checked 5 November 2026) says "The camera snaps both the front and back simultaneously" inside "the same two-minute window" everyone gets. BeReal's help centre adds that you can retake as many times as you like before posting, and that you can post after the 2 minutes are up, "but your friends will know you posted late".

Getting the photo out is the limited part. BeReal's "Share & Download Memories" article describes one route: open your profile, tap "View all my Memories", open the BeReal, tap the three-dot menu, then "the option to add to photos". The same article says "It is not possible to download all your Memories at once." BeReal's help centre does not document a way to save the selfie and the back photo as two separate files.

How do you take a BeReal-style photo on Snapchat?

Open the Snapchat Camera, tap the Dual Camera icon in the Camera toolbar, try the layouts and take a Snap. Snapchat Support says Dual Camera "allows you to capture content using the front and back cameras at the same time", and the flip button swaps which camera is the main view. Picture in picture is the BeReal look. The selfie window is part of the image, so you can't move it or remove it afterwards. The full walkthrough is in how to take a Snap with the front and back camera.

Can the iPhone Camera app take a BeReal-style photo?

No, not as a photo. Apple's iPhone User Guide puts Dual Capture in Video mode: open Camera, select Video, tap the camera-controls button, then tap Dual Capture. Apple describes it as a way to record yourself and the action in front of you at the same time. On the seven supported models, record two seconds, pause on the best frame and take a screenshot. The model list and limits are in which iPhones have Dual Capture.

Can Instagram take a BeReal-style photo?

Instagram's Dual is a recording feature. Meta's 21 July 2022 announcement says "You can record using your phone's front and back cameras simultaneously" with Dual in the Instagram camera. As with Dual Capture, a still means a screenshot.

How do you get the selfie and the back photo as two separate photos?

Use ContentCam's Front & Back mode (iPhone, or an Android phone that can run both cameras at once):

  1. Open ContentCam and switch to Front & Back mode.
  2. Frame the rear shot.
  3. Press the photo shutter once. ContentCam saves the selfie and the rear photo as two separate photos.

Two separate photos mean you can post the back photo clean, put the selfie in any corner later, or use the two as slides 1 and 2 of a carousel. Front/Back photo works on every iPhone that runs iOS 18 or later, including iPhone 15 and iPhone 16, which don't have Apple's Dual Capture. On Android, ContentCam's Front & Back mode takes photos the same way, on phones that can run both cameras at once.

Where do BeReal and Snapchat win over a camera app?

  • Cost. BeReal and Snapchat's Dual Camera are free. Photo capture in ContentCam is a Premium feature.
  • The social part. BeReal's point is that your friends post in the same 2-minute window. A camera app has no feed and no friends.
  • Speed. Snapchat gives one finished image in one tap. Two separate photos need a layout step if you want them combined.
  • Android. BeReal runs on Android. ContentCam's Front & Back photos need an Android phone that can run both cameras at once.

FAQ

Can you take a BeReal photo without the notification?

Yes. BeReal's help centre says you can post a BeReal any time after the notification, but your friends will see that it was posted late. To take a front and back photo with no notification at all, use Snapchat's Dual Camera or a front and back camera app such as ContentCam on iPhone.

Can you save the BeReal selfie and the back photo separately?

BeReal's help centre does not document it. The one download route it describes is opening a Memory, tapping the three-dot menu and choosing the option to add it to Photos, and it says you cannot download all your Memories at once.

Does the iPhone 17 Camera app take a BeReal-style photo?

Not as a photo. Apple's iPhone User Guide documents Dual Capture only in Video mode, on iPhone 17, iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone Air, iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max and iPhone Duo. Record a short clip and screenshot the best frame if you want a still.

Which app saves the front and back photo as two separate photos on iPhone?

ContentCam. In Front & Back mode, one press of the photo shutter saves the selfie and the rear photo as two separate photos, on any iPhone running iOS 18 or later, from iPhone XS and XR onwards. Photo capture in ContentCam is a Premium feature.

Is there a free way to take a BeReal-style photo on Android?

BeReal itself is free on Android. Snapchat's help centre still says Dual Camera will come to Android soon, so Snapchat is not an option there yet. ContentCam's Front & Back mode takes two separate photos on Android phones that can run both cameras at once (Premium).
