# DualShot Recorder Review (2026): Tested by Hand

Source: https://contentcam.app/blog/dualshot-recorder-review/

DualShot Recorder 3.1.2 tested on 13 November 2026: takes lost on lock or force-close, 4K 60 saved at 38-39 fps, a centre watermark on free, no update since June, and paid users left without support.

By Jimmy, developer of ContentCam · 13 November 2026 · Tested 13 November 2026 on DualShot Recorder 3.1.2

**Short answer:** DualShot Recorder does the basic job of saving a vertical and a horizontal video from one take, but I wouldn't trust it with anything you can't film again. In my test, a take was lost every time the phone locked, the app closed or a call came in. 4K at 60 fps saved at 38 to 39 fps. Free recordings carry a DualShot logo in the middle of the frame. The app hasn't been updated since 5 June 2026, and people who paid for it once say on its App Store page, its Instagram and its own support group that their access disappeared and nobody answered.

## What does DualShot Recorder do well?

It's simple to start. There's no account, one tap records, and each take saves as two files with matching names ending in "_Portrait" and "_Landscape". The Advanced tier lists a good spread of features for the price of a subscription: 4K, MOV, bitrate control, Apple Log, timelapse and a teleprompter. When a purchase doesn't restore, the app has a second route, "Restore with Order Number", which takes the order number from your Apple receipt. Its website says the app has no analytics SDKs and that footage stays on your device.

## What did I find when I tested it?

On 13 November 2026 I installed DualShot Recorder 3.1.2 from the App Store on an iPhone, recorded on the free Basic tier, then started the Advanced free trial and tested again. Every frame rate below is the number iPhone Photos shows in the file's info panel, which you can check on your own clips.

### 1. Takes are lost if anything interrupts them

I interrupted takes five ways: locking the phone, swiping to the home screen, force-closing the app, opening Siri, and taking an incoming call. Each time, the recording was gone. Nothing was saved and there was nothing to recover. I also found no way to reach the app's recordings from the Files app, so if a take doesn't make it to Photos you can't go looking for it.

This matches what users report. A 1-star review from Australia (11 April 2026) reads: "I filmed an interview last night and after I pressed finish, it said error and everything was lost." In the app's own Telegram support group, one user's iPhone Storage screen showed 48.46 GB of DualShot "Documents & Data" after an interview that never appeared in Photos (23 October 2026). Another asked on 3 November where a finished recording had gone. If that's happened to you, don't delete the app: [here's where a missing video usually is and what not to delete](https://contentcam.app/blog/iphone-video-not-in-photos-after-recording/).

### 2. 4K at 60 fps doesn't record at 60 fps

With Advanced unlocked, I set 4K at 60 fps and recorded two takes. The four files (portrait and landscape from each take) saved at 38.19, 38.39, 39.08 and 39.23 fps, and motion in them looks choppy. A file that should hold 60 frames each second holds about 39, so slow motion and smooth pans are out. Set to 4K 60 fps. Photos reports 38.19 and 39.23 fps.

### 3. The free tier: a centre watermark, and settings that don't match what's saved

Free recordings are 1080p with a DualShot Recorder logo across the middle of the picture, not tucked in a corner, so you can't crop it out. DualShot's website calls it a "small DualShot watermark".

The frame rate is confusing. DualShot's website says the free tier records at 30 fps, and in the settings 30 is the only frame rate without a lock. The paywall lists 24, 25, 50 and 60 fps as Advanced features. Yet my settings screen showed 25 fps selected on the free tier, and my free takes saved at 24 fps (one at 23.93). The camera settings also reset between takes: frame rate went back to 24 fps and lens mode back to Standard, so you'd have to set them again before every recording.

**In ContentCam:** the free version has no watermark at all, records 1080p at 24, 25 or 30 fps, keeps your settings between takes, and has no time limit per clip. A free take: logo over the picture, saved at 23.93 fps. Free tier: 25 fps highlighted, though it shows a lock. Only 30 fps is unlocked.

### 4. Cinematic stabilisation puts the preview 2 seconds behind

Cinematic stabilisation smooths footage by looking a little ahead in time, which delays the picture. In DualShot Recorder that delay shows up in the picture-in-picture preview: with Cinematic on, the small preview ran about 2 seconds behind what the camera was seeing. You end up framing a shot you can't see yet. Even with stabilisation off, the picture-in-picture looked low in frame rate and laggy.

**In ContentCam:** the preview stays in real time with cinematic stabilisation on. It's the only dual-format app I've tested that manages it, and on iPhone cinematic stabilisation is included free.

### 5. Manual controls only at 1x, and confusing to reach

Manual ISO and white balance only work in the Advanced lens mode, which the app describes as "Manual ISO & white balance (1x zoom only)". The Standard mode covers 0.5x and up, but with "manual ISO unavailable". So there are no manual controls on the ultra-wide lens. When I tapped the controls while already at 1x in Standard mode, the app said "Switch to Advanced lens for manual controls", which only makes sense once you know it means a different mode, not a different zoom. Manual ISO and white balance: Advanced lens mode, 1x only.

### 6. Focus, zoom and layout

- **No plain tap to focus.** A tap locks focus and exposure (AE/AF lock) straight away; there's no lighter tap that just focuses. The focus square also didn't appear where I tapped.
- **No zoom buttons or zoom dial.** Zoom is pinch only, which is hard to do smoothly mid-take.
- **The picture-in-picture can be moved but not resized.** There's no pinch or size button.
- **The record button sits left of centre.** A small thing, but it's the one button you use every take. A 17 August 2026 App Store review mentions it too.
The dashed line marks the centre of the screen.

### 7. One camera at a time

DualShot Recorder records the front *or* the back camera, not both, and I found no mode that films the vertical and the horizontal through two different lenses at once. Its listing says "Front and back camera support", which means you can pick either camera; the developer's site puts it as "Front or back camera, your call".

**In ContentCam:** Front & Back records the selfie and rear cameras at the same time as two separate files, and Dual Lens films the vertical and the horizontal through two different lenses, each framed on its own. Both work in 4K on supported iPhones.

### 8. It asks for full access to your photo library

Saving a video to Photos only needs permission to *add* to your library. DualShot Recorder asks for "full access to your Photo Library", which lets an app see every photo and video you have (mine: 2,522 photos and 1,273 videos). Its own explanation is that it needs it "to save recorded portrait and landscape video files", which add-only access already covers. When I chose Limit Access and gave it three photos, it asked again every time I opened the app. DualShot Recorder asks for full access to read your library. My photos are blurred.

**In ContentCam:** it only asks to add videos to Photos, so it can't see your library and never asks for more.

## Is DualShot Recorder still updated?

Not since June. DualShot Recorder came out on 27 March 2026 and had a run of updates through spring: version 3.0 on 2 May added the teleprompter, manual ISO and stabilisation, and 3.1 on 26 May moved the app to subscriptions. Then 3.1.2 shipped on 5 June 2026 with the notes "Bug fixes and performance improvements", and that's the version on the App Store more than five months later.

That matters for a camera app because iPhones and iOS keep changing underneath it. In early October, a user asked under the app's Instagram posts: "Will there be stability updates for the app on ios27? Every time I try to use the front facing camera the app crashes."

## What happened to people who paid for DualShot Recorder once?

DualShot Recorder launched as a one-time purchase. When version 3.1 moved it to subscriptions in late May, the developer's website promised that everyone who bought the one-time version keeps every Advanced feature "free, forever" and that the app "detects your prior purchase via your Apple ID" automatically. That's a fair policy. The problem is what happened when it didn't work.

Since early May I count at least 17 App Store reviews in the US, UK, Australia and Canada from people who paid the one-time price and were later shown a watermark, asked to subscribe, or found Advanced locked. Several say they then emailed support and heard nothing back:

- "I have submitted the receipt ID & screenshots of the purchase as instructed by the app but it doesn't recognise the purchase. I enrolled the support email numerous time but got no response." (UK, 16 July 2026)
- "I haven't been able to restore my purchase … I've tried restoring my purchase multiple times and have also contacted support several times through email but the issue still hasn't been resolved." (Australia, 6 July 2026)

## So is DualShot Recorder worth it in 2026?

For short, casual clips you could easily reshoot, it works: open it, record, get two files. For anything that matters (an interview, a client shoot, a one-off moment), I wouldn't use it. The takes don't survive an interruption, the 4K 60 setting doesn't deliver 60 fps, the free tier's watermark sits where you can't crop it out, and with no update since June and no visible support, the bugs here are unlikely to be fixed soon. If you paid for it once and lost access, try Restore Purchases, then "Restore with Order Number" in Settings; if neither works, you can ask Apple for a refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).

I'll say this plainly: I'm a solo developer too, and I know how hard it is to keep an app going alone. I'm not writing this to pile on. But people paid for DualShot Recorder, some of them for a version they were told was theirs for life, and they deserved an answer.

## DualShot Recorder FAQ

### Is DualShot Recorder free?

It's free to download. The free Basic tier records portrait and landscape at 1080p, as MP4, up to 1 minute per clip, with a DualShot logo in the centre of the frame. The developer's site says 30 fps; in my test on 13 November 2026, free takes saved at 24 fps. 4K, other frame rates, MOV, manual ISO and white balance, cinematic stabilisation, Apple Log, timelapse and the teleprompter need the Advanced subscription.

### Is DualShot Recorder still being updated?

Its last update was version 3.1.2 on 5 June 2026, listed as "Bug fixes and performance improvements". As of 13 November 2026 there has been no update since.

### Can DualShot Recorder record 4K at 60 fps?

It offers the setting on the Advanced tier, but in my test on 13 November 2026 the files saved at 38.19 to 39.23 fps, with choppy motion. Check the frame rate of your own test clip in iPhone Photos (swipe up on the video).

### Why did my DualShot Recorder recording disappear?

In my test, a take was lost whenever the phone locked, the app was closed or force-closed, Siri opened, or a call came in. Users also report finished recordings that never reached Photos while the app's storage grew by tens of gigabytes. Check Settings > General > iPhone Storage > DualShot Recorder, and don't delete the app, because deleting it deletes anything it's holding.

### I bought DualShot Recorder once. Why is it asking me to subscribe?

DualShot Recorder moved to subscriptions in version 3.1 (26 May 2026). The developer's website says one-time buyers keep Advanced for free, detected automatically through your Apple ID. If that hasn't happened, try Restore Purchases, then "Restore with Order Number" in Settings using the order number on your Apple receipt. Many buyers report that neither worked and support didn't reply; in that case you can request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

### Is DualShot Recorder on Android?

No. It's iPhone only; the developer's website says an Android version is in development. ContentCam is on iPhone and Android.
