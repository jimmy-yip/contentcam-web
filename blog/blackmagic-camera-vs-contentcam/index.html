Short answer: if you post the same video to TikTok, Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts and to horizontal YouTube, ContentCam is the better pick, because one take gives you a 9:16 vertical file and a 16:9 horizontal file. Can Blackmagic Camera record vertical and horizontal at the same time?

No. Blackmagic Design's product page says you "can shoot in 16:9 or vertical aspect ratios", and that choice is made before you press record. Neither the product page nor the App Store listing for version 3.5.3 (released 23 October 2026) describes a mode that saves both from one take. For a clip headed to TikTok and to YouTube, that means filming twice or cropping.

Two takes never match, and cropping a 16:9 frame down to 9:16 keeps about 32% of the width, so the vertical version is soft and badly framed. (I broke down the maths in how to record portrait and landscape at the same time.)

ContentCam records both at once. One take saves a true 9:16 file and a true 16:9 file, each framed on its own, in HD on the free tier or 4K with Premium.

ContentCam's record and review screens: both framings while you shoot, then a side-by-side review of each file.

Can Blackmagic Camera record the front and back cameras at once?

Not according to Blackmagic Design. Its product page describes the lens control as "Select a lens for your shot", picking one of the rear lenses or the front lens. Its multi-camera features sync several separate phones or Blackmagic cameras; they don't run two cameras on one phone.

ContentCam's Front & Back mode records the selfie camera and the rear camera at the same time as two separate files. On iPhone it works on every model that runs iOS 18 or later, including iPhone 15 and iPhone 16, which don't have Apple's own Dual Capture. On Android, ContentCam checks whether the phone's camera system can run both cameras at once and only offers the mode where it works. Afterwards, the Combine editor can turn the two files into one picture-in-picture or split-screen video, with the originals kept. Free users get 5 Front & Back recordings; Premium makes it unlimited. On iPhones with an ultra-wide camera (iPhone 11 and later, except iPhone SE and 16e), Dual Lens also records wide and ultra-wide together.

Which app is quicker for daily TikTok, Reels and YouTube content?

In my view, ContentCam, because it's built around that job. Blackmagic Camera's own listing pitches "the professional features you need for feature film, television and documentaries": shutter angle, timecode, LUTs, Blackmagic Cloud sync. A talking-head creator posting four times a week needs different things:

  • A teleprompter, so you're not cutting around forgotten lines. ContentCam has one built in; Blackmagic Camera's listing doesn't mention one.
  • Both formats per take, so one recording feeds TikTok, Reels, Shorts and YouTube.
  • No account. Free ContentCam records both formats in HD, with unlimited takes, no ads and no watermark, and your footage stays on your phone.

Who should use Blackmagic Camera instead? Filmmakers who grade log footage in DaVinci Resolve or need Apple ProRes on iPhone.
