ContentCam APK and Mod APK: Is There One? (2026)

By Jimmy, developer of ContentCam · 4 October 2026

Short answer: there is no official ContentCam APK and no official ContentCam mod APK. I build ContentCam, and I have only ever released it in two places: the App Store for iPhone and Google Play for Android. Any "ContentCam mod APK" or "Content Cam APK" download on another website was rebuilt by someone else.

The good news for anyone searching for one: ContentCam is free to download, the free version has no watermark, and premium is priced per country, so it may cost less where you live than you expect. The details are below.

Is there an official ContentCam APK download?

No. ContentCam does not publish an APK file on this website or anywhere else. On Android, the only official source is the Google Play listing ContentCam: Dual Camera Video, package name app.contentcam.android, which runs on Android 8.0 or later. On iPhone, the only official source is the App Store, and ContentCam needs iOS 18 or later.

Google Play installs the app for you, so you never need to handle an APK file yourself. If a site offers "ContentCam APK latest version" as a direct download, that file did not come from me.

What is a mod APK, exactly?

A mod APK is an Android app that someone has unpacked, changed and packed back up. For paid apps, the usual change is patching out the purchase check so premium features work without paying. The modder then signs the rebuilt file with their own key, because only the original developer holds the original one, and uploads it to a download site.

Android's own documentation explains why that signature matters. The Android Open Source Project says "every app that is run on the Android platform must be signed by the developer", and that the package manager checks the signature when an app is installed. A modded ContentCam APK can only be signed by whoever modified it, so the signature tells you nothing about what is inside.

What are the risks of a ContentCam mod APK?

The risks are practical, and they apply to any modded app, not only ContentCam:

  • The developer did not sign it. You are trusting an anonymous uploader with an app that asks for your camera and microphone.
  • It can carry malware. Google's Google Play Protect help page says Play Protect checks your device "for potentially harmful apps from other sources" and may warn you about them or remove them.
  • It does not get updates. Google's app signing guide says that when Android installs an update, "it compares the certificate(s) in the new version with those in the existing version". A modded build has a different certificate, so official updates and bug fixes from Google Play will not install over it.
  • It can stop working. A patched build is frozen at whatever version was modded. When Android, your phone or the store changes, nobody fixes it.

One more thing worth knowing. The official ContentCam collects no data: no account, no analytics and no tracking SDKs, and the Play Store Data Safety label reads "no data collected". A rebuilt APK carries none of that promise, because you cannot see what the modder added.

How much does ContentCam premium cost?

ContentCam premium comes three ways, and all three include the same features:

  • Lifetime, one payment with no renewal (Family Sharing on iOS)

I do not quote a single price here because there is no single price. The App Store and Google Play set prices per region and show them in your own currency, so premium in India, Indonesia or Brazil is priced differently from premium in the US. Open ContentCam or its store listing to see the real figure for your country. Payments go through Apple or Google, and ContentCam never sees your payment details.

If you cancel a subscription, you keep premium until the end of the period you paid for, then the app drops back to the free tier. Your recordings and projects stay where they are.
