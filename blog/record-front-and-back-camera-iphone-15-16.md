## Does the iPhone 15 or iPhone 16 have Dual Capture?

No. Apple lists Dual Capture, the Camera app's front and back video mode, for seven phones: iPhone 17, iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone Air, iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max and iPhone Duo. Every iPhone 15 model and every iPhone 16 model, including iPhone 16e, is missing from that list. The details of what Dual Capture does on the newer phones are in [which iPhones have Dual Capture and its limits](https://contentcam.app/blog/iphone-17-dual-capture-limitations/).

## Can an iPhone 15 or iPhone 16 record both cameras at once?

Yes. The iPhone 15 and iPhone 16 support Apple's multi-camera capture for apps, so a third-party camera app can run the front camera and the back camera together; only the Camera app lacks the mode. The apps differ in one way that matters: one finished video or two separate files.
