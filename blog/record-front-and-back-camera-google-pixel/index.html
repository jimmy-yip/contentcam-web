Can You Record the Front and Back Camera at the Same Time on a Google Pixel?

By Jimmy, developer of ContentCam · 9 October 2026 · Updated 5 November 2026

Not in Pixel Camera. Google's own camera app has no front and back video mode on any Pixel, including the Pixel Fold models. A Google Pixel can record both cameras at once only in a third-party app, and only if that Pixel's camera system reports that the front and back cameras can run together.

Disclosure: I build ContentCam, a camera app with a Front & Back mode on Android, so I'm not neutral. The facts about Pixel Camera below come from Google's Pixel help pages and the Android developer documentation, checked 5 November 2026. For Samsung Galaxy phones and every other Android brand, see recording front and back on Android.

Does Pixel Camera have a front and back camera mode?

No. Google's Pixel Camera Help page "Record a video on your Pixel phone" covers Video mode, Slow motion (down to 1/8x), Time Lapse (5x to 120x, plus Auto), Night Sight Time Lapse, Cinematic Blur, Cinematic Pan, 10-bit HDR and Video Boost, with video up to 4K at 60fps on models that support it. None of those modes records the selfie camera and the rear camera together, and the page has no picture-in-picture or split-screen option.

Is Dual screen preview on the Pixel Fold the same thing?

No. Dual screen preview uses two screens, not two cameras. Google's "Explore your Pixel Fold camera" page says: "On Pixel Fold models, you can use both the inside and outside screen simultaneously," which "allows your subject to see themselves while you record the video." Google lists it for the Pixel Fold, Pixel 9 Pro Fold, Pixel 10 Pro Fold and Pixel 11 Pro Fold. The video still comes from one camera.

Is Add Me on the Pixel 9 and Pixel 10 a dual camera feature?

No. Google's blog describes Add Me as a way to "combine two pictures taken during the same session and in the same scene" so the photographer ends up in the group photo. Add Me launched with the Pixel 9, and the Google Store lists it on "all Pixel 9 and Pixel 10 phones". Add Me works on still photos and does not run the front camera and the back camera at the same time.

Which Google Pixel phones can record front and back in an app?

Google doesn't publish a model list, and I won't guess one. Support depends on the phone's camera hardware, and Android tells apps about it in two ways:

  • CameraManager.getConcurrentCameraIds(), added in Android 11 (API level 30), returns the sets of cameras that "support configuring camera device sessions concurrently".
  • The feature flag android.hardware.camera.concurrent (FEATURE_CAMERA_CONCURRENT) means "the device's main front and back cameras can stream concurrently".

If a Pixel reports neither, no app using Android's official camera route can record both cameras on it. The Android Open Source Project documentation explains why: concurrent streaming depends on the phone's image signal processor (ISP) resources, which the camera system has to split between the two cameras.

How do you check if your Pixel supports front and back recording?

  1. Easiest: install a camera app that reads the concurrent camera report and see if the front and back option appears. ContentCam checks the phone and only offers Front & Back where it works.
  2. With a computer: turn on USB debugging, connect the Pixel and run adb shell pm list features. If the list includes android.hardware.camera.concurrent, the main front and back cameras can stream together.

What quality do you get recording both cameras on a Pixel?

Less than a normal Pixel Camera video. Android's concurrent streaming guarantees go up to s1440p per camera, which the Android source docs define as 1440p (1920 x 1440), with combinations that pair 720p (1280 x 720) and 1440p. Google's CameraX 1.3 announcement puts the cap at "720p or 1440p, depending on the device." ContentCam's Front & Back mode records HD at up to 30fps on Android.

FAQ

Does Pixel Camera have a dual camera or front and back video mode?

No. None of the video features in Google's Pixel Camera Help records the front camera and the back camera at the same time, on any Pixel model (checked 5 November 2026).

Does Dual screen preview on the Pixel Fold record both cameras?

No. Dual screen preview shows the viewfinder on the Pixel Fold's inside and outside screens so your subject can see themselves. It uses two screens, not two cameras.

Is Add Me on the Pixel 9 and Pixel 10 a front and back camera feature?

No. Add Me combines two still photos of the same scene so the photographer appears in the group shot. It does not run the front and back camera together.

Which Pixel phones can record front and back in a third-party app?

Google does not publish a list. A Pixel can do it only if it reports a front and back pair through getConcurrentCameraIds() (Android 11 and later) or the android.hardware.camera.concurrent feature. ContentCam checks this and only shows Front & Back on phones that report support.

What quality is front and back recording on a Pixel?

Lower than a normal Pixel video. Android guarantees concurrent streams up to 1440p (1920 x 1440) per camera. ContentCam's Front & Back mode records HD at up to 30fps on Android.
