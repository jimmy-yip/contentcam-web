How to Record the Front and Back Camera at the Same Time on Samsung Galaxy

By Jimmy, developer of ContentCam · 10 October 2026 · Updated 5 November 2026

To record the front and back camera at the same time on a Samsung Galaxy, use Dual recording in the Samsung Camera app: on One UI 8.5 it sits in Video mode under Quick controls, on older software under More. Dual recording is the new name for Director's View. For your face and the scene as two separate files, the Samsung Camera app only does that on the Galaxy S22 and S23; on other Galaxy phones, use an app such as ContentCam's Front & Back mode.

Disclosure: I build ContentCam, so I'm not neutral. Every Samsung claim below comes from Samsung's own support pages, and every other app's details from its Google Play listing, checked 5 November 2026. For Android phones in general, including Pixel, see recording front and back at the same time on Android.

What happened to Director's View on the Galaxy S24 and S25?

Samsung renamed Director's View to Dual recording. Samsung's support page "Use Director's View and Dual recording on your Galaxy phone" says Director's View came to select devices running One UI 3.1 and higher, and that "Director's View is not available on the Galaxy S24 series or S25 series." A second Samsung page, "Director's View Missing in Camera?" (updated 1 July 2026), explains why: "The Director's view menu has been renamed to Dual recording in newer Samsung Galaxy devices."

Which Samsung Galaxy phones have Dual recording?

Samsung lists five groups as supporting Dual recording:

  • Galaxy S24 series
  • Galaxy S25 series
  • Galaxy Z Fold series
  • Galaxy Z Flip series
  • Galaxy A series (select models, which Samsung doesn't name)

The newer phones get more options. Samsung lists FHD or UHD at 30fps, Front + Rear Camera, and Rear + Rear Camera, and notes that the Galaxy S23 series was limited to FHD at 30fps and Front + Rear Camera.

How do you turn on Dual recording on One UI 8.5?

Samsung's US support page says that from One UI 8.5, Dual rec "has been integrated into the Video menu":

  1. Open the Camera app and tap Video.
  2. Tap the Quick controls icon (the four circles).
  3. Tap Dual recordings (a square with a smaller square in the corner) and record.

To make Dual rec its own mode again, tap Quick controls, then the gear, then Camera Assistant, and turn on Dual rec under Additional modes. Samsung India's page labels it "Camera Assistant (Downloadable)", so the phone may download it first. Dual rec then shows under More. On software before One UI 8.5, Samsung's steps are Camera, More, then Dual recording (or Director's View on older models).

Does Samsung Dual recording save two separate files?

Only on the Galaxy S22 and S23 series. Samsung's support page says S22 and S23 owners can tap the Download icon before recording to save the selfie video and the rear video as separate files, and that "this setting is not currently available on any other device besides the S22 series and S23 series." Everywhere else, Dual recording saves one video with picture-in-picture or split screen baked in, chosen before you press record.

How do you check if a Galaxy can run both cameras in other apps?

Android 11 (API level 30) added CameraManager.getConcurrentCameraIds(), which returns the camera pairs that can run together, and the feature flag android.hardware.camera.concurrent, which Android Developers documentation defines as "the device's main front and back cameras can stream concurrently." Without a front and back pair, no third-party app can record both through that official route. Google's CameraX documentation puts the ceiling for two cameras at "720p or 1440p", depending on the phone.

The easy check is an app that tests the phone: ContentCam only offers Front & Back where it works, and DuoCam's listing says it "checks your phone hardware on launch". With a computer, adb shell pm list features shows android.hardware.camera.concurrent if the phone has it.

How do you record front and back on a Galaxy in ContentCam?

  1. Install ContentCam from Google Play.
  2. Switch the recording mode to Front & Back. If the mode isn't offered, your Galaxy can't run both cameras at once for apps.
  3. Choose vertical or horizontal for the rear camera, then record once. The selfie and the rear camera save as two separate files, recorded in sync.

FAQ

Is Director's View gone on the Galaxy S24 and S25?

Only the name. Samsung renamed Director's View to Dual recording, and lists Dual recording for the Galaxy S24 series, Galaxy S25 series, Galaxy Z Fold series, Galaxy Z Flip series and select Galaxy A models.

Where is Dual recording on One UI 8.5?

From One UI 8.5, Samsung moved Dual rec into Video mode: open Camera, tap Video, tap Quick controls (four circles), then tap Dual recordings.

Can a Samsung Galaxy save the front and back camera as two separate files?

In the Samsung Camera app, only the Galaxy S22 and S23 series, per Samsung. Other Galaxy models save Dual recording as one video. Apps such as ContentCam's Front & Back mode and DuoCam save separate files on Galaxy phones that can run both cameras at once.

How do I check if my Galaxy can run the front and back camera at the same time?

The phone has to report Android's concurrent camera feature, android.hardware.camera.concurrent, added in Android 11. ContentCam and DuoCam check the phone for you and only offer front and back recording where it works.
