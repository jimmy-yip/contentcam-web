## Does ContentCam need internet or an account to record?

No. ContentCam records with no internet connection and no account, on iPhone and Android, and your footage stays on your phone. That covers Front & Back (selfie and rear camera as two files) and both formats from one lens (a 9:16 file and a 16:9 file from the same take).

One thing does need a connection: buying or restoring Premium, because that goes through the App Store or Google Play. Premium is yearly, monthly or a one-time lifetime purchase, with the same features on all three.

## What about Dualgram, DualShot Recorder and Apple's Dual Capture?

- **Dualgram:** the Dualgram FAQ says videos save "directly into your device's Photo library" and that the free app "includes advertisements, watermarks, and certain locked features". Neither the listing nor the FAQ says whether recording works offline.
- **DualShot Recorder:** records vertical and horizontal from one take, but with one camera at a time. Its listing's "Front and back camera support" means you choose either camera, not both together.
- **Apple Dual Capture:** part of the built-in Camera app, so no download or account, but only on iPhone 17, 17 Pro, 17 Pro Max, Air, 18 Pro, 18 Pro Max and iPhone Duo, as one combined file at up to 30fps.

## How do you prepare a dual camera app for a flight, hike or concert with no signal?

1. Install the app and buy any upgrade on Wi-Fi first. App Store and Google Play purchases need a connection.
2. Record one test clip at home in airplane mode, so you know the app runs with no signal.
3. Free up storage. ContentCam's Front & Back mode saves two files per take, so each take uses roughly twice the space of a single-camera clip at the same settings.
