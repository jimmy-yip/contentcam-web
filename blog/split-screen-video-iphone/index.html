How to Make a Split Screen Video on iPhone (2026)

By Jimmy, developer of ContentCam · 4 November 2026

The free way to make a split screen video on iPhone is iMovie, Apple's own editor: start a movie project with your first clip, scroll to where the second clip should start, tap Add Media, pick the second video, tap the More button and choose Split Screen. The two clips play side by side, and the Swap button stacks them one above the other instead. CapCut does the same job with its Overlay tool. If you want both halves recorded at the same moment, your face on one half and the view on the other, you record the front and back camera together and combine them afterwards, which is covered further down.

How do you make a split screen video on iPhone with iMovie?

These steps come from Apple's iMovie User Guide page Add video overlay effects in iMovie on iPhone, checked 4 November 2026 against iMovie 3.0.6. iMovie is free on the App Store.

  1. Open iMovie and start a movie project with the first clip. This becomes the main clip in the timeline.
  2. Scroll the timeline so the playhead, the white vertical line, sits where the second clip should start.
  3. Tap the Add Media button, tap Video, choose a category, then tap the second video.
  4. Tap the More button and tap Split Screen.

Apple's guide says that by default "the two clips appear side by side, equal in size". To adjust the layout, tap the split screen clip in the timeline so it outlines in yellow, then use the controls that appear in the viewer:

  • Swap turns the side by side layout into one clip above the other.
  • Line adds or removes the dividing line between the clips.
  • Zoom Control lets you pinch and drag to choose which part of a clip shows inside its half.
  • Overlay changes the effect to Picture in Picture, Cutaway or Green/Blue Screen if split screen isn't working.

Trim the second clip in the timeline to set how long the split lasts. Where the overlay clip ends, the video goes back to the main clip at full frame.

How do you make a split screen video on iPhone with CapCut?

In CapCut you build the split by hand with the Overlay tool. CapCut's own guide, How to Add Overlays on CapCut (checked 4 November 2026), describes it: add your background video, tap Overlay, select the second video and tap Add overlay, then pinch to resize it and drag to position it.

For a split, resize the background clip to fill the top half of the frame and the overlay to fill the bottom half, or left and right for a widescreen video. The advantage over iMovie is control: you can make the halves unequal, add a third clip, and set the canvas to 9:16 before you start.

Should a split screen be top and bottom or side by side?

Top and bottom for TikTok, Reels and Shorts; side by side for YouTube. The arithmetic is what decides it.

On a vertical split, put the half that matters most on top. TikTok, Reels and Shorts lay the caption, username and buttons over the bottom of the frame, so the lower half is the one that gets covered. A reaction video usually works best with the thing being reacted to on top and the face below, kept clear of the bottom edge.

How do you record a split screen video on iPhone while filming?

The iPhone Camera app has no split screen mode. The closest built-in option is Dual Capture on iPhone 17, iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone Air, iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max, and Apple's guide illustration shows it as a window in the top left corner of the frame rather than a split. The details are in which iPhones have Dual Capture, and where it stops.

Two ways to get a real split while recording:

  • Snapchat Dual Camera. Snap's newsroom post of 29 August 2022 lists four layouts: vertical, horizontal, picture in picture and cutout. The vertical and horizontal layouts are split screens, recorded live. Snapchat Support lists Dual Camera for iPhone XS, XS Max, XR or later. More in how to take a Snap with the front and back camera.
  • Record front and back as two files, then combine. ContentCam, which I build, records the selfie camera and the rear camera together in Front & Back mode and saves each as its own file. Since version 2.8, the iPhone app has a Combine button on Front & Back takes that turns the two files into one video, picture-in-picture or a 50/50 split. A vertical rear shot gives a 1080×1920 video split top and bottom; a horizontal rear shot gives a 1920×1080 video split side by side. The original two files stay as they are.

The second route costs an extra step, and it buys you the choice later. The same take can be a split screen on TikTok, a face-in-the-corner version on Reels, and a straight rear camera shot with cutaways to your face on YouTube.

How do you fix the audio on a split screen video?

Pick one clip's audio and turn the other one down. Two clips filmed in the same room carry the same sound a fraction of a second apart, which comes out as an echo, and two unrelated clips playing together are just noise. In iMovie and CapCut, select the clip you don't want to hear and lower its volume to zero. If both halves were recorded together, keep the audio from whichever microphone was closer to the person talking.

Common questions

Can you split screen two videos on iPhone without an app? The Photos app has no split screen tool. iMovie is Apple's free option and the closest thing to built-in.

Can you make the split unequal, like two thirds and one third? iMovie's Split Screen gives two equal halves, and Apple's guide lists no control for resizing them. Use CapCut's Overlay and size each clip by hand.

How do you put two videos side by side on iPhone? Use iMovie's Split Screen overlay, which is side by side by default. Tap Swap to stack them instead.

Does Dual Capture do split screen? No. Apple documents Dual Capture as the front camera in a window over the rear camera's view, saved as one video.

Can you split screen on Android? Yes, with CapCut's Overlay tool, which works the same way on Android. For recording, some Samsung Galaxy phones have Director's View with split layouts; see recording front and back at the same time on Android.
