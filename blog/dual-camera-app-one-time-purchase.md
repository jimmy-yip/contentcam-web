## Why does a weekly subscription cost more over a year?

A weekly plan bills 52 times a year, so a small weekly price adds up past most yearly plans. The US maths from the listings above:

- DoubleTake weekly at $1.49: 52 × $1.49 = $77.48 a year, about 3.9 times DoubleTake's own $19.99 yearly plan.
- MixCam weekly at $1.99: 52 × $1.99 = $103.48 a year.
- DualShot monthly at $1.99: 12 × $1.99 = $23.88 a year, against $9.99 for DualShot's yearly plan.
- Dualgram's $29.99 one-time PRO equals about 3.3 years of Dualgram's $8.99 yearly plan.

On DoubleTake, 4 of the last 25 US App Store reviews (January 2025 to September 2026) complain about the subscription, from "They lock you into a weekly subscription model" (1 star, 9 February 2026) to "No lifetime purchase option" (1 star, 30 March 2025). 8 of those 25 mention crashes, freezes or failed recordings, and DoubleTake has not been updated since 10 March 2025. For MixCam, 6 of the last 25 US reviews (June 2023 to October 2026) complain about the price or the paywall.

A one-time purchase only wins if you keep using the app. For a single trip, a free tier or one month costs less.
