Where I give an opinion from using it, I flag it as mine. If anything's out of date, email me and I'll fix it.

Does DualShot Recorder put a watermark on free videos?

Yes. DDJR Productions' own website says DualShot Recorder's free Basic tier records at 1080p and 30fps, caps each clip at 60 seconds and adds a DualShot watermark; the Advanced subscription removes it. That watermark is the most common complaint in recent reviews: 4 of the last 50 US App Store reviews (22 June to 28 October 2026) are about watermarked footage, including buyers who paid for version 2. One 1-star review (1 October 2026) says "you've spent hours filming footage with a watermark that can't be removed". ContentCam adds no watermark and shows no ads, on the free tier or Premium.

Has DualShot Recorder lost recordings?

Some users report it: in the same 50 reviews, 3 describe recordings that stopped or never saved, and 2 describe audio out of sync. A 1-star review from 18 July 2026 reads "constantly crashing and not saving/restoring recorded content". DualShot Recorder's last update, 3.1.2 on 5 June 2026, is listed only as "Bug fixes and performance improvements", and the last version that added features was 3.0 on 2 May 2026 (teleprompter, manual ISO, stabilisation). Whichever app you choose, record a test take before a paid shoot.

Can ContentCam record two cameras at once?

Yes, in two ways. Dual Lens records the wide and ultra-wide cameras at the same time on iPhone 11 and later, so the portrait and landscape files can be genuinely different framings. Front & Back mode records the selfie and rear cameras at the same time as two separate files, on every iPhone that runs iOS 18 (including iPhone 15 and iPhone 16) and on Android phones that support it; the free tier includes 5 Front & Back recordings. In single-lens mode, ContentCam records the sensor's full 4:3 frame and cuts both formats from it, so the landscape keeps the full width instead of being a centre strip. DualShot Recorder records one camera at a time: the "front and back camera support" in its listing means either camera can be used, and its site puts it as "Front or back camera, your call".

ContentCam's record and review screens: both framings while you shoot, then a clean side-by-side review.

Which app feels more finished? (my opinion)

I'm biased, but ContentCam is the more complete tool: Dual Lens, Front & Back, the full manual panel (exposure, white balance, ISO and shutter), and a project, scene and take workflow with automatic file naming. DualShot Recorder felt more basic in my testing: the picture-in-picture preview lagged and the record button sat slightly off-centre, which a 17 August 2026 App Store review also mentions.

Can you still buy DualShot Recorder once?

Not as a new buyer. DualShot Recorder launched as a one-time purchase; version 3.1 (26 May 2026) made it free to download with optional subscriptions, and its App Store listing now shows only "Advanced Annual" and "Advance Monthly" in-app purchases. Version 2 buyers keep Advanced, per DDJR Productions. ContentCam keeps a one-time lifetime purchase alongside yearly and monthly plans, and all three get the same features.
