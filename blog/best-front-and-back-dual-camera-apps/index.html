Which front and back camera app is reliable for long recordings like concerts?

Reliability comes up again and again in this category's App Store reviews. DoubleTake: 9 of its 12 most recent US reviews (all from 2026) report crashes, the app not opening, or it no longer saving its two files on iOS 26. One 1-star review on 1 January 2026 says it "Crashed out and did not record in the middle of new year count down." DualShot Recorder: at least 15 of its last 250 reviews describe footage that was lost or didn't save. Whatever app you use:

  • Turn on Do Not Disturb or a Focus mode so a notification can't interrupt the camera.
  • Do a 2-minute test at the venue and play back both cameras.
  • Check free storage and heat. Two cameras write about twice the data of one. Long recordings without overheating.
