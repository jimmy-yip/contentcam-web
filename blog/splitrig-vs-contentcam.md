# SplitRig vs ContentCam (2026): Which Front and Back Camera App Should You Use?

Source: https://contentcam.app/blog/splitrig-vs-contentcam/

SplitRig saves one combined front and back video on iPhone with iOS 26. ContentCam saves the front and back camera as two separate files, records vertical and horizontal from one take, and runs on iOS 18 and Android. A checked comparison.

By Jimmy, developer of ContentCam · 5 October 2026 · Updated 12 November 2026

Use ContentCam instead of SplitRig if you want the front and back camera as two separate files you can edit, a vertical and a horizontal video from the same take, Android, manual ISO, shutter and white balance, or an iPhone that isn't on iOS 26. SplitRig saves one finished picture-in-picture or split-screen video, runs only on iPhone, and needs iOS 26 or later.

Disclosure: I build ContentCam. Every SplitRig claim below comes from SplitRig's App Store listing (version 1.1.11, released 10 October 2026) and splitrig.com, checked 5 November 2026.

## Is SplitRig free?

Yes. SplitRig's App Store listing says "Free forever, with no subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads, and no account." ContentCam is free to download with 5 free Front & Back recordings, then Premium; ContentCam has no ads and no watermark on any plan.

## Does SplitRig save separate front and back files?

SplitRig's listing and site describe one combined video. The App Store description promises "one smooth, perfectly synced video", the splitrig.com FAQ says it records "into one ready-to-post video file", and the features table lists "One synced file". Neither mentions saving the front camera and the back camera as separate files.

ContentCam's Front & Back mode always saves two separate files that start at the same moment. Combine can turn them into a picture-in-picture or split-screen video in the app, and the two originals stay in place.

## Which is better for editing later, SplitRig or ContentCam?

ContentCam, because two separate files give an editor choices that one combined file has already made. With the selfie file and the scene file apart you can:

- cut between full-screen face and full-screen scene in CapCut, Final Cut Pro or DaVinci Resolve;
- move, resize or remove the inset per shot instead of living with where it sat during recording;
- colour-grade the face and the scene separately;
- re-run Combine with a different layout for TikTok and for YouTube from the same take.

A SplitRig file has the inset or split baked in, so changing the layout means recording again.

## Can SplitRig record vertical and horizontal at the same time?

No such mode appears in SplitRig's listing or on splitrig.com; SplitRig saves the orientation you shoot. ContentCam's main mode records a 9:16 file for TikTok, Reels and Shorts and a 16:9 file for YouTube from one lens in one take, in HD or 4K, at 24, 25, 30, 50 or 60fps.

## Does SplitRig need full access to your photos?

SplitRig asks for it. On first use, SplitRig 1.1.11 shows the iOS prompt "SplitRig would like full access to your Photo Library", explaining that full access "also creates a dedicated SplitRig album". You can tap Limit Access instead. SplitRig's own privacy policy describes the permission as "Photo Library (Add-only)" and says "We never read your existing library", so the prompt and the policy don't match. ContentCam only asks for add-only access: it can save your recordings to Photos but can't see anything already in your library.
