DoubleTake Not Working? What Still Records Two Cameras at Once (2026)

By Jimmy, developer of ContentCam · 12 October 2026 · Updated 5 November 2026

If DoubleTake won't open or won't save its two files, the replacement I recommend is ContentCam: its Front & Back mode records the selfie and rear cameras at once as two separate files, on any iPhone running iOS 18 (iPhone XS, XR and newer) and on Android. On an iPhone 17 or newer, Apple's Dual Capture is a free fallback if one combined video is enough. DoubleTake's current version, 3.1.2, shipped on 10 March 2025, six months before iOS 26, and hasn't been updated since.

Disclosure: I build ContentCam, so I'm not neutral. Every claim about the other apps comes from their App Store listings, reviews or developer sites, checked 5 November 2026. The full feature comparison is in DoubleTake vs ContentCam.

Is DoubleTake broken on iOS 26?

The developer hasn't said. DoubleTake is published by Bending Spoons Operations SpA, which bought Filmic in 2022 (see what happened to Filmic Pro). Neither the App Store listing nor Filmic's DoubleTake page mentions iOS 26, and that page still describes version 2.0 for iPhone 11 and 12.

DoubleTake's own US App Store reviews point one way. Of the 23 reviews posted since version 3.1.2 shipped, 12 report DoubleTake crashing, freezing or not opening, and 6 complain about the subscription or refunds. A 1-star review from 26 January 2026 reads: "No longer will save an a and b file since iOS 26." Reviews on 21 and 25 March 2026 say DoubleTake no longer opens on iOS 26, one on an iPhone 13 Pro Max running iOS 26.3.1. I haven't tested DoubleTake on iOS 26 myself.

Why is DoubleTake not working on my iPhone?

  • DoubleTake won't open. The most common complaint since iOS 26; a 25 March 2026 reviewer says reinstalling didn't fix it.
  • DoubleTake crashes mid-take and loses the clip. A 1-star review from 1 January 2026: "Crashed out and did not record in the middle of new year count down."
  • DoubleTake freezes on the start screen offline. A 15 December 2025 review says it froze out of mobile range and worked again on Wi-Fi.
  • Only the composite file saves. The 26 January 2026 review says picture-in-picture still records but separate A and B files don't.
  • DoubleTake records one camera only. The listing restricts multi-cam to iPhones up to the iPhone 13 series (plus iPad Pro 2018 and newer); other devices record a single camera.

What's the closest replacement for DoubleTake's separate A and B files?

ContentCam's Front & Back mode works the way DoubleTake used to: one press records the selfie camera and the rear camera as two separate files, ready to cut in CapCut or Final Cut. Afterwards, the Combine editor can render the pair into one picture-in-picture or split-screen video and keeps the originals. It runs on iPhone 15 and 16, which lack Dual Capture, and on Android phones that can run both cameras at once. There are no ads and no watermark, free or Premium.

Apple's Dual Capture saves one combined video, so there are no separate angles to re-cut.

FAQ

Is DoubleTake still being updated?

No. Version 3.1.2, from 10 March 2025, is still DoubleTake's current version as of 5 November 2026. The publisher is Bending Spoons Operations SpA.

Does DoubleTake work on iOS 26?

Bending Spoons hasn't said. 12 of the 23 US App Store reviews since version 3.1.2 report crashes, freezing or DoubleTake not opening, and 2026 reviews describe it failing on iOS 26. I haven't tested it on iOS 26 myself.

What is the free built-in alternative to DoubleTake?

Dual Capture in Apple's Camera app, on iPhone 17, 17 Pro, 17 Pro Max, Air, 18 Pro, 18 Pro Max and iPhone Duo. It records one video with the front camera inset, with no separate files. iPhone 16, 16e and 17e don't have it.

Which app saves the front and back camera as separate files on iPhone?

ContentCam's Front & Back mode saves the front and back camera as two separate files on iPhone (iOS 18) and Android, with no ads or watermark.

Can DoubleTake record in 4K?

No. The DoubleTake App Store listing says 1080p is the maximum resolution for its multi-cam capture, at 24, 25 or 30fps.
