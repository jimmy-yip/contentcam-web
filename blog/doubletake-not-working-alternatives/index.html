DoubleTake Not Working? Alternatives That Still Record Two Cameras at Once (2026)

By Jimmy, developer of ContentCam · 12 October 2026 · Updated 5 November 2026

If DoubleTake won't open or won't save its two files, the quickest replacement is Dual Capture in Apple's Camera app on an iPhone 17 or newer, and on any other iPhone, a third-party front-and-back app such as ContentCam, RØDE Capture, SplitRig or Dualgram. DoubleTake's current version, 3.1.2, shipped on 10 March 2025, six months before iOS 26 came out, and it hasn't been updated since.

Disclosure: I build ContentCam, so I'm not neutral. Every claim about DoubleTake and the other apps below comes from their App Store listings, their reviews or the developer's own site, checked 5 November 2026. The full feature comparison is in DoubleTake vs ContentCam.

Is DoubleTake broken on iOS 26?

The developer hasn't said. DoubleTake is published by Bending Spoons Operations SpA, which bought Filmic in 2022 (the background is in what happened to Filmic Pro). Neither the DoubleTake App Store listing nor Filmic's DoubleTake product page mentions iOS 26, and the product page still describes version 2.0 for iPhone 11 and 12 series devices.

DoubleTake's own US App Store reviews tell a consistent story. One from 26 January 2026 reads: "No longer will save an a and b file since iOS 26." Reviews on 21 and 25 March 2026 say DoubleTake no longer opens on iOS 26, including on an iPhone 13 Pro Max running iOS 26.3.1. Two August 2026 reviews report DoubleTake not opening on an iPhone 16 Pro and an iPhone 14 Pro, without naming an iOS version. I haven't tested DoubleTake on iOS 26 myself.

Why is DoubleTake not working on my iPhone?

  • DoubleTake won't open. The most common complaint since iOS 26. One reviewer (25 March 2026) says deleting and reinstalling DoubleTake didn't fix it.
  • DoubleTake freezes on the start screen with no signal. A reviewer (15 December 2025) found DoubleTake froze at startup while hiking out of mobile range and worked again back on Wi-Fi. If you're offline, connect and try again.
  • Only the composite file saves. The 26 January 2026 review says picture-in-picture still records but separate A and B files don't. If you need two angles today, the picture-in-picture composite is the fallback.
  • DoubleTake records one camera only. The listing says multi-cam is restricted to a list of iPhones that ends at the iPhone 13 series (plus iPad Pro 2018 and newer), and that other devices record a single camera. That list hasn't been updated since.

What's the closest replacement for DoubleTake's separate A and B files?

ContentCam's Front & Back mode, RØDE Capture and Dualgram all list separate files. ContentCam's Front & Back mode saves the selfie camera and the rear camera as two separate files from one press, ready to cut in CapCut, Final Cut or DaVinci Resolve. Afterwards, ContentCam's Combine editor can render the pair into one picture-in-picture or split-screen video and keeps the originals.

ContentCam also has Dual Lens on iPhone, which records the wide and ultra-wide cameras at once (iPhone 11 and later, not iPhone SE or iPhone 16e), for people who used DoubleTake for two rear angles.

Where does DoubleTake still beat the alternatives?

  • DoubleTake runs on iPad and needs only iOS 14.1. ContentCam needs iOS 18 and has no iPad version.
  • DoubleTake lets you pair any two cameras, including the telephoto, per its listing.

And where the others beat ContentCam: Dual Capture is free on the phones that have it and records 4K at 30fps, where ContentCam's Front & Back mode records HD. SplitRig and RØDE Capture are free for front and back, while ContentCam's Front & Back mode is Premium after 5 recordings.

FAQ

Is DoubleTake still being updated?

Not since 10 March 2025. DoubleTake is still listed on the App Store, published by Bending Spoons Operations SpA, but version 3.1.2 from 10 March 2025 is still the current version as of 5 November 2026.

Does DoubleTake work on iOS 26?

Bending Spoons hasn't said either way. DoubleTake App Store reviews since January 2026 report the app not opening on iOS 26, or no longer saving separate A and B files. I haven't tested DoubleTake on iOS 26 myself.

What is the free built-in alternative to DoubleTake?

Dual Capture in Apple's Camera app, on iPhone 17, iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone Air, iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max and iPhone Duo. Dual Capture records up to 4K Dolby Vision at 30fps as one video with the front camera inset. iPhone 16, iPhone 16e and iPhone 17e don't have it.

Which DoubleTake alternative saves the front and back camera as separate files?

ContentCam's Front & Back mode saves the front and back camera as two separate files on iPhone and Android. RØDE Capture lists a Separate Files option, and Dualgram says it saves both originals plus the combined video.

Can DoubleTake record in 4K?

No. The DoubleTake App Store listing says 1080p is the maximum resolution for its multi-cam capture, at 24, 25 or 30fps.
