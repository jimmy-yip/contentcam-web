Open the Snapchat Camera, tap the Dual Camera icon in the Camera toolbar, pick a layout, then take the Snap. The catch is the phone: Snapchat's help centre lists Dual Camera for iPhone XS, XS Max, XR or later, and still says the Android version is coming "soon". How do you take a Snap with the front and back camera?

Snapchat's Dual Camera takes three taps, according to Snapchat Support:

  1. Open the Snapchat Camera screen (the Chat camera works too).
  2. Tap the Dual Camera icon in the Camera toolbar down the side of the screen.
  3. Try the layouts, then tap the capture button for a photo Snap or hold it for a video Snap.

Snapchat Support says the flip button swaps which camera is the primary view. Snap's launch post says you can add music, Stickers and Lenses, and use Dual Camera for Snaps, Stories and Spotlight videos.

What layouts does Snapchat's Dual Camera have?

Snapchat's Dual Camera has four layouts, per Snap's newsroom post of 29 August 2022: vertical and horizontal split screen, picture in picture (the BeReal-style look) and cutout. You pick the layout before you capture. The result is one Snap that cannot be pulled apart later. ContentCam works the other way round: record first, then try picture-in-picture or split screen in Combine.

Is there a front and back camera filter on Snapchat?

Snapchat has no filter called front and back camera. The "front and back camera snapfilter" people search for is Dual Camera, a toolbar mode rather than a Lens.

Some Lenses use both cameras. Snap's Lens Studio documentation says "the Dual Camera feature is not supported on all devices", so on an unsupported phone a two-camera Lens may ask for a photo or video from your camera roll instead.

Which phones have Snapchat Dual Camera?

Snapchat Dual Camera works on iPhone XS, XS Max, XR and later, and Snapchat Support says it "is available for everyone to use", not only Creators. Android is the gap: Snap's 29 August 2022 launch post promised "Android support in the coming months", and on 5 November 2026 Snapchat Support still says Dual Camera "will be available on Android soon", more than four years later.

Why is the Dual Camera icon missing on Snapchat?

  • You are on Android. Dual Camera is not on Android yet, on any phone.
  • The iPhone is older than an iPhone XS or XR. Those models are not on Snapchat's list.
  • Snapchat is out of date. Update it from the App Store and reopen the Camera.

How do you do a front and back Snap on Android?

On Android, record the front and back camera outside Snapchat, then post the video to Snapchat from your camera roll. Snap's Memories announcement (6 July 2016) covers using a Camera Roll video in a new Story.

  • ContentCam (any brand). ContentCam's Front & Back mode works on Samsung, Pixel and other Android phones that can run two cameras at once, records HD at up to 30fps, and saves the selfie and the scene as two separate files. Combine them into picture-in-picture or split screen in the app, or post either file alone.
  • Samsung Galaxy. Samsung's Camera app has Dual recording on select phones running One UI 3.1 and later, with the layout fixed before you record. Samsung says Director's View is not on the Galaxy S24 or S25 series; on those phones the mode is called Dual recording. Recording front and back on Android has the detail.
  • Google Pixel. Pixel Camera has no front and back mode, so a Pixel needs a third-party camera app such as ContentCam.

Record in ContentCam when the clip has a second life. Because the two cameras stay separate, one take can become a Snap, a TikTok with the face inset in another corner, and a YouTube cutaway. Snapchat Dual Camera suits a quick reply or Story that never leaves Snapchat.
