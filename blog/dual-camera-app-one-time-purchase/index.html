By Jimmy, developer of ContentCam · 15 October 2026 · Updated 5 November 2026

DoubleTake and MixCam list weekly plans. Every price here is the US App Store price in US dollars, read from each app's own listing and checked 5 November 2026.

Why does a weekly subscription cost more over a year?

A weekly plan bills 52 times a year, so a small weekly price adds up past most yearly plans. The US maths from the listings above:

  • DoubleTake weekly at $1.49: 52 × $1.49 = $77.48 a year, about 3.9 times DoubleTake's own $19.99 yearly plan.
  • MixCam weekly at $1.99: 52 × $1.99 = $103.48 a year.
  • DualShot monthly at $1.99: 12 × $1.99 = $23.88 a year, against $9.99 for DualShot's yearly plan.
  • Dualgram one-time at $29.99 equals about 3.3 years of Dualgram's $8.99 yearly plan.

A one-time purchase only wins if you keep using the app. For a single trip, a free tier or one month costs less.

Which free dual camera app has no ads?

SplitRig, if your iPhone runs iOS 26: the SplitRig listing says it is free forever with no in-app purchases and no ads, and it saves one synced picture-in-picture or split-screen video. Twofold's free version has no ads either, but it adds a watermark until you buy Twofold Pro. CamDuo and Dualgram show ads on the free tier.

Where do the other apps beat ContentCam?

  • Price: SplitRig costs nothing, and CamDuo and Twofold are single small payments built only around front and back recording.
  • A finished video at the tap: Twofold and SplitRig save a picture-in-picture video when you stop. ContentCam's Front & Back mode saves two separate files, and you combine them afterwards in the Combine editor.
  • Resolution: Dualgram lists up to 4K at 60fps. ContentCam's Front & Back mode records HD.

FAQ

Is there a dual camera app with no subscription at all?

Yes. SplitRig's App Store listing says it is free with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads, but it needs iOS 26. CamDuo ($4.99 one-time) and Twofold ($6.99 one-time) also have no subscription plan, per their listings (US prices, checked 5 November 2026).

Which dual camera apps charge weekly?

DoubleTake by Bending Spoons lists weekly plans from $0.99 to $9.99, and MixCam by Mixcord lists a $1.99 weekly plan, on their US App Store listings checked 5 November 2026.

How much does a $1.49 weekly subscription cost in a year?

$77.48 in the US, because a year has 52 weekly charges. That is about 3.9 times DoubleTake's own $19.99 yearly plan.

Does ContentCam have a one-time purchase?

Yes. ContentCam Premium is a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, and all three include the same features. There is no weekly plan, and no ads or watermark on any plan. Prices vary by country and are shown in the App Store and Google Play.

Is a one-time purchase always cheaper than a yearly plan?

Only if you keep using the app. Dualgram's $29.99 one-time Unlock PRO costs the same as about 3.3 years of its $8.99 yearly plan, so the one-time purchase pays off from the fourth year.
