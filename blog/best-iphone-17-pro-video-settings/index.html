Best iPhone 17 Pro Video Settings (2026)

By Jimmy, developer of ContentCam · 13 November 2026

For most iPhone 17 Pro and iPhone 17 Pro Max video, set Record Video to 4K at 30 fps, keep Formats on High Efficiency, leave Apple ProRes off, turn HDR Video off if you post to social apps, and turn Auto FPS off and Lock Camera and Lock White Balance on. Switch to ProRes Log only when you plan to colour grade, and to 4K at 120 fps only for slow motion. The two Pro models share the same camera system, so every setting below applies to both. The specs come from Apple's tech specs pages for the iPhone 17 Pro and iPhone 17 Pro Max, checked 13 November 2026.

The iPhone 17 Pro settings below are the ones I would use for creator work: talking heads, walk-and-talks, b-roll and product shots. Menu paths are from Apple's iPhone User Guide (Change video recording settings).

Should you record ProRes or Apple Log 2 on iPhone 17 Pro?

Only if you colour grade in an editor like DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut Pro. ProRes Log (Apple Log 2 on the iPhone 17 Pro) looks flat and grey straight out of the camera and needs a LUT or a grade before anyone sees it. For a clip you post from the phone, HEVC with HDR Video off looks better and takes a fraction of the space. Apple's About Apple ProRes on iPhone page (checked 13 November 2026) gives the limits:

On the iPhone 17 Pro, Apple lists ProRes as HDR or Log, with no SDR option. Every iPhone 17 Pro starts at 256 GB, so the stricter 128 GB limits on older Pro models do not apply. An external drive must be formatted exFAT, not password protected, connected with a USB 3 cable rated 10 Gbps or faster, and draw no more than 4.5 W. Turn ProRes on in Settings > Camera > Formats, then choose ProRes Log or ProRes HDR from the resolution and frame rate controls in Video mode. ProRes RAW is listed for the iPhone 17 Pro too, but Apple says it is "enabled by compatible apps", so the built-in Camera app does not record it.

Should you film in 4K 60 or 4K 120 on iPhone 17 Pro?

4K at 30 fps for normal speed, 4K at 60 fps for action or for a 2x slow down, and 4K at 120 fps only for slow motion b-roll. The iPhone 17 Pro records 4K Dolby Vision at 24, 25, 30, 60, 100 and 120 fps, but 100 and 120 fps work on the Fusion Main (1x) camera only. A 120 fps clip on a 30 fps timeline plays at a quarter speed. 100 fps does the same job on a 25 fps timeline in PAL countries. Every step up in frame rate needs more light per second, so 120 fps indoors looks noisier than 30 fps in the same room. More on choosing frame rates in 24fps vs 30fps vs 60fps for social media.

Which iPhone 17 Pro lens is best for video?

The 1x Fusion Main camera for most shots. The iPhone 17 Pro has three 48 MP back cameras, and the 1x camera has the widest aperture and the only frame rates above 60 fps:

The 2x lens is the flattering one for a talking head: a 48 mm view with the same ƒ/1.78 aperture as the 1x camera, so it costs no light. The 4x and 8x lenses at ƒ/2.8 let in less than half the light of ƒ/1.78, so save them for daylight. Turn Lock Camera on before using any of them, otherwise the iPhone 17 Pro can swap to a different camera when the subject moves closer, and the colour and sharpness change mid shot. Video digital zoom goes up to 15x.

What are the best front camera video settings on iPhone 17 Pro?

4K at 30 fps, the same as the back camera. The iPhone 17 Pro front camera is an 18 MP Center Stage camera that records 4K Dolby Vision at 24, 25, 30 or 60 fps, and ProRes up to 4K at 60 fps with external recording. For a talking head on the front camera, face a window or a soft light rather than having it behind you, and turn Lock White Balance on so the skin tone holds.

What resolution does Dual Capture record on iPhone 17 Pro?

Up to 4K Dolby Vision at 30 fps, front and back cameras together, saved as one combined video with the front camera inset over the back camera. If you want the two cameras as separate files to edit, place or crop yourself, a third-party app has to do it. ContentCam's Front & Back mode records each camera to its own file, in 4K on supported iPhones since version 2.9. The limits of Apple's version are in iPhone 17 Dual Capture limitations.

Are iPhone 18 Pro video settings any different?

The same settings work on the iPhone 18 Pro. The iPhone 18 Pro tech specs (checked 13 November 2026) list the same 4K frame rates, ProRes limits and Dual Capture, with three differences for video: Cinematic mode goes up to 4K at 60 fps instead of 30, digital zoom goes up to 24x instead of 15x, and Apple lists pro controls for lens aperture, shutter speed, white balance and a histogram. Auto FPS also works in 4K on the iPhone 18 Pro, which is one more reason to turn it off before a 4K shoot.

Common questions

Is iPhone 17 Pro video better in HEVC or H.264? HEVC. Choose High Efficiency in Settings > Camera > Formats. Most Compatible records H.264 at roughly twice the file size for the same picture. The full comparison is in iPhone High Efficiency vs Most Compatible.

How much storage does iPhone 17 Pro 4K video use? In my own HEVC clips, 4K at 30 fps ran 169 to 275 MB a minute, against about 60 MB for 1080p. 60 and 120 fps use more, and Apple says ProRes files are up to 30 times larger than HEVC.

Can the iPhone 17 Pro record ProRes 4K 60 to its internal storage? No. Apple limits internal ProRes to 4K at 30 fps. 4K at 60 and 120 fps in ProRes need an external drive over USB-C.

Does the iPhone 17 Pro Max have better video than the 17 Pro? No. Apple lists the same cameras, lenses and video formats for both. The Pro Max has a bigger screen and battery, which helps on long shoots.

Should I use Action mode on iPhone 17 Pro? Only for running, cycling or fast walking. Action mode records up to 2.8K at 60 fps, so it gives up 4K for the extra stabilisation.
