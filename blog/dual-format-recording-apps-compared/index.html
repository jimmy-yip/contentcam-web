Where DualShot Recorder is genuinely ahead

Credit where due, because this is the part comparison posts usually skip:

  • Apple Log. If you colour grade seriously, DualShot has Log capture today and ContentCam doesn't yet.
  • Price. Its annual plan undercuts ContentCam's. If budget is the deciding factor and you're on iPhone, that's a real argument. Check both store listings for the current figures in your region.
  • Name recognition. It's the one people have heard of. It went viral first, largely off the back of its creator's existing audience. If you just want the app everyone's talking about, that's it.

One caveat worth knowing on price: DualShot launched as a one-time purchase and has since moved to subscription only, with no lifetime option anymore. ContentCam keeps a one-off lifetime option alongside its subscriptions. For a fuller head-to-head, see DualShot Recorder vs ContentCam.

The rest of the category

ReFrame, DuoShot, Dual Recorder and several others shipped in the same wave with very similar names and listings. I haven't used each one long enough to review them fairly, so I won't pretend to. Two things to check on any of them before paying: whether the landscape file is a genuine wide capture or a crop of the portrait (the listing usually won't say, but a test recording will show it immediately), and what the App Store privacy label discloses.

The one technical question that separates these apps

Ask this of any dual-format app: where does the landscape file's width come from?

If the app records a portrait stream and crops a 16:9 strip from it, the landscape version shows less of your scene than the portrait does, upscaled. If the app records the sensor's full 4:3 frame and crops both formats from that, the landscape file genuinely shows more left-and-right than the portrait. ContentCam does the latter (explained here); for any other app, record one take and compare the two files side by side.

The cheapest way to decide: both leading apps have free tiers. Record the same scene in each and look at the two landscape files.
