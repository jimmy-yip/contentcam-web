How can you tell if the landscape file is a real wide shot?

Ask this of any dual-format app: where does the landscape file's width come from?

If the app records a portrait stream and crops a 16:9 strip from it, the landscape version shows less of your scene, upscaled. BiCam's site describes this approach. If the app records the sensor's full 4:3 frame and crops both formats from that, the landscape file genuinely shows more left-and-right than the portrait. ContentCam's Single Lens mode does the latter (explained here); for any other app, compare one take's two files side by side.

What else do people ask about dual-format apps?

Why not just crop a vertical video to widescreen? Because cropping only removes picture. A full-width 16:9 cut from a 9:16 frame keeps about 32% of the height, so anything outside the centre band is gone. Recording both formats at capture is the only way to keep the full frame in each.
