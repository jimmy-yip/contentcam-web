How to Do Dual Camera on TikTok (2026)

By Jimmy, developer of ContentCam · 6 November 2026 · Updated 6 November 2026

To do dual camera on TikTok, record the front and back camera together in another app and upload that clip to TikTok, because TikTok's own camera records one camera at a time. ContentCam's Front & Back mode, which I build, is my pick for most phones: it works on any iPhone running iOS 18 and on Android phones that can run two cameras at once, saves your face and the scene as two separate files, and its Combine editor turns them into one picture-in-picture or split-screen video for TikTok.

Disclosure: I build ContentCam, so I'm not neutral. Every claim about TikTok, Apple, Snapchat and Instagram below comes from their own help pages or announcements, checked 6 November 2026.

Does TikTok have a dual camera mode?

No, not in the TikTok camera. TikTok's Camera tools help page lists six tools: Flip, Speed, Video length, Timer, Flash and Zoom. Flip changes "the camera direction", and during a take you can "double-tap the video to switch to the front or back camera". That gives you the two cameras in turn, never on screen together.

TikTok Now, launched 15 September 2022, did use "your device's front and back camera" for "a 10-second video or a static photo". TechCrunch reported on 27 June 2023 that TikTok was discontinuing it.

Are Duet, Stitch or Green Screen a dual camera on TikTok?

No. All three combine your recording with a video that is already on TikTok, not with your phone's second camera:

  • Duet: TikTok's help centre says a Duet "contains two videos in a split screen that play at the same time", yours beside another creator's.
  • Stitch: lets you "combine a video on TikTok with one you're creating", using part of the other creator's video.
  • Green Screen Duet: TikTok's 12 May 2021 announcement describes the Duetted video playing behind you as you record.

How do you post a front and back video on TikTok with ContentCam?

  1. Install ContentCam and switch to Front & Back. On Android, the mode only appears on phones that can run both cameras together (front and back on Android).
  2. Press record once. The selfie camera and the rear camera save as two separate files from the same take.
  3. Open Combine and pick picture-in-picture (drag and pinch the inset) or split screen. Trying layouts is free; saving is Premium. With the rear camera vertical, the iPhone Combine video comes out 1080×1920.
  4. In TikTok, tap Add post +, then Upload, and pick the combined video. TikTok allows uploads up to 60 minutes, against 10 minutes for videos recorded in TikTok.

What settings should a dual camera video for TikTok use?

  • Shape: 9:16 vertical. TikTok's own video ad specs list vertical 9:16 as the recommended format, at 540×960 or more.
  • Frame rate: 30fps for talking and reactions, 60fps only if you'll slow the clip down. Reasons in 24 vs 30 vs 60fps for social media.
  • Know the caps. Apple's Dual Capture tops out at 30fps. ContentCam's Front & Back records HD at up to 60fps on iPhones that support it (60fps is Premium) and up to 30fps on Android.

When is another option better than ContentCam for TikTok?

  • You own an iPhone 17, Air, 18 Pro or Duo and post the clip as shot: Dual Capture is free, already in the Camera app, and records up to 4K. ContentCam's Front & Back records HD. Dual Capture's limits are in which iPhones have Dual Capture.
  • You want a quick clip at no cost on an older iPhone: Snapchat's Dual Camera is free from iPhone XS and XR.
  • You only react to other TikToks: Duet and Green Screen need nothing else installed.

ContentCam fits when your phone has no Dual Capture (iPhone 16 and earlier, iPhone 16e, iPhone 17e, every Android phone), or when you want two separate files to edit.

FAQ

Does TikTok have a dual camera mode?

Not in the TikTok camera. TikTok's Flip tool switches between the front and back camera one at a time. TikTok Now used both cameras, but TikTok discontinued it in June 2023.

Can you use the front and back camera at the same time on TikTok?

Not while recording inside TikTok. Record both cameras in ContentCam, Apple's Dual Capture, Snapchat or Instagram, then tap Upload in TikTok.

How do you do dual camera on TikTok with an iPhone 15 or iPhone 16?

Neither phone has Apple's Dual Capture, so use a third-party app. ContentCam's Front & Back mode runs on iOS 18 or later, saves two separate files, and Combine turns them into one video for TikTok.

Is Duet the same as dual camera on TikTok?

No. A Duet puts your video beside another creator's TikTok in a split screen. It never uses your phone's other camera.
