## Where is the Dual camera on Instagram?

Dual lives in the Instagram camera. Meta's newsroom post of 21 July 2022, which launched Dual alongside new Reels tools, describes it in one line: "You can record using your phone's front and back cameras simultaneously to share another perspective using the Dual feature in the Instagram camera." On 6 November 2026 I found no Instagram Help Center page for Dual and no list of supported phones, and the Instagram App Store listing (version 454.1, released 4 November 2026) does not mention Dual.

## Why is Instagram Dual missing or not working?

Meta doesn't say. Two causes are checkable:

- **An old Instagram version.** Dual arrived in July 2022, and Instagram on the App Store now needs iOS 16.3 or later.
- **A phone that can't run two cameras at once.** Snapchat, which does publish a list for its Dual Camera, supports iPhone XS, XS Max, XR and later. On Android, the camera system itself has to allow the front and rear cameras to stream together, and not every phone does.

Account type, region and staged rollouts get blamed online, but Meta has published nothing linking Dual to any of them.

## How do you fix Instagram Dual not showing up?

1. Update Instagram from the App Store or Google Play, then force-close and reopen Instagram.
2. Open the Instagram camera for a Reel and check the full tool list for Dual.
3. Still no Dual? Meta documents no switch to turn Dual on, so record in another app and upload the clip.

Meta does not publish a list. For comparison, Snapchat's Dual Camera needs iPhone XS, XS Max, XR or later, and on Android the camera system has to allow the front and rear cameras to run together.
