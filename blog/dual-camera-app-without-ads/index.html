Why does an ad in a camera app cost you the shot?

A full-screen ad covers the viewfinder and the record button. A reaction, a goal or a sunset happens once, and while an ad is on screen you can't frame or start the take.

Store reviews of the ad-supported apps report exactly this. 6 of the 12 most recent US App Store reviews of MixCam - Dual Video Recorder complain about ads, all of them 1 star, including one from 15 April 2026: "It won’t let you complete a video without an ad interrupting!!" On Google Play, "Dual Camera : Front & Back Cam" (Backs Infotech) is rated 2.0 stars from 4.3K reviews, and a 1-star review from 11 December 2025 opens: "The app is bloated with ads."

How do you tell if a camera app has ads before installing it?

  1. Google Play: look for "Contains ads" under the app name. Google's Play Console Help says "Apps that contain ads show a 'Contains ads' label on their store listing".
  2. App Store: scroll to Information, then Age Rating. Apple asks developers to disclose features such as advertising there, and apps that answer yes show "Contains Advertising" under the rating.
  3. In-app purchases: on either store, a purchase named "Remove Ads" means the free version shows ads. "Remove Watermark" means free exports carry the app's logo.
  4. Privacy label: "Third-Party Advertising" on an App Store privacy label is a weaker signal. It also covers apps that only measure their own marketing elsewhere, so check the Age Rating line first.
