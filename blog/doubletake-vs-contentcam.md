## Does DoubleTake work on iOS 26?

Not reliably, going by its App Store reviews. Of the 12 US reviews posted in 2026 (checked 5 November 2026), 9 report crashes, freezes, the app not opening, or the separate A and B files no longer saving. Three of them tie the problem directly to iOS 26:

- **26 January 2026, 1 star:** "No longer will save an a and b file since iOS 26".
- **21 March 2026, 2 stars:** an iPhone 13 Pro Max on iOS 26.3.1 can't get DoubleTake to open.
- **25 March 2026, 2 stars:** "After IOS 26 updates no longer opens", from a reviewer on a yearly plan.

Later reviews report the same on newer phones: on 22 August 2026 DoubleTake didn't get past its logo on an iPhone 16 Pro, and on 28 August 2026 it no longer opened on an iPhone 14 Pro. I haven't tested DoubleTake on iOS 26 myself. Version 3.1.2 predates iOS 26, so if you rely on DoubleTake, test a recording before an important shoot.

## What should you know about DoubleTake before a shoot?

**1080p is the listed ceiling.** The listing states 1080p at high bitrate encoding. A 1-star review from 6 September 2026 says DoubleTake shot 1080p even with 4K selected. If you crop or reframe in the edit, starting at 1080p costs you room you can't get back.

**The multicam device list ends at the iPhone 13 series.** Nothing newer is named, and the listing still says "Per iOS 15 capabilities".

**Recordings can be lost in a crash.** Reviews from 29 August 2025 and 1 January 2026 describe DoubleTake crashing mid-recording and losing the clip.
