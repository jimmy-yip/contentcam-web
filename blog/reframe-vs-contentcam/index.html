Does ReFrame have ads?

Yes. ReFrame's App Store page shows "Contains Advertising" under Age Rating, checked 5 November 2026. The listing doesn't say where the ads appear or whether Pro removes them. ContentCam shows no ads on any plan, including the free version. If ads are the deciding factor, ContentCam shows none on any plan.

Is there a lifetime option?

Yes, in both apps. ReFrame's in-app purchase list includes Pro Lifetime alongside Pro Weekly, Pro Monthly and Pro Yearly. ContentCam Premium is monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase, and all three include the same features. ContentCam has no weekly plan. More one-time options are in dual camera apps with a one-time purchase.

What does ReFrame's free version include?

ReFrame's listing says the app is free to use at 1080p and 30fps, and that ReFrame Pro adds 4K, 60fps, HDR, Face Tracking, captions, premium filters and unlimited exports. Free exports in ReFrame are therefore capped; the listing doesn't give the number. ContentCam's free version records both formats from one lens in HD with unlimited takes, no watermark and no account. 4K in both formats is part of ContentCam Premium.

Can ReFrame record front and back at the same time?

ReFrame's listing doesn't describe it. The line "Every lens on your device, front and back" sits under lens and quality options, which reads as choosing a lens, not running the selfie and rear cameras together. ContentCam's Front & Back mode records both cameras at once as two separate files, on every iPhone that runs iOS 18 (iPhone 15 and iPhone 16 included) and on Android phones that can run two cameras. Combine turns the pair into one picture-in-picture or split-screen video afterwards, keeping the originals. The first 5 Front & Back recordings are free.

Which is better on Android?

ContentCam, because ReFrame: Dual Camera Recorder is not on Android. A Google Play app called "ReFrame: Dual Shot 4K Camera" is published by a different developer, AVTUNES, so it is a separate product from RedSpark Labs' iPhone app. ContentCam on Android records 9:16 and 16:9 from one take, and Front & Back at up to 30fps on phones that support it.

How reliable is ReFrame, according to its reviews?

ReFrame's App Store ratings are high overall, but its written reviews show a reliability pattern. Of 143 written reviews across 20 App Store countries (read 5 November 2026), 8 describe the camera opening to a black or white screen, often after paying for Pro, 8 describe recordings that failed to save or were cut off, and 7 describe freezing at 2K or 4K. One Malaysian reviewer (18 July 2026, 2 stars) wrote: "I only manage to record once… Black screen, can't see anything". A Korean reviewer (22 April 2026, 3 stars) reported "videos are not saved due to a 'converting error'". Every ReFrame version note since 1.0.1 reads only "Bug fixes and performance improvements".
