DoubleTake vs ContentCam: An Honest Comparison (2026)

By Jimmy, developer of ContentCam · 7 September 2026 · Updated 5 November 2026

If DoubleTake has stopped opening or saving its two files on iOS 26, the closest replacement is ContentCam: its Front & Back mode records your reaction and the scene at the same time as two separate files, which the Combine editor can turn into one picture-in-picture or split-screen video afterwards, on iPhone and Android. ContentCam also records a 9:16 vertical and a 16:9 horizontal file from one take, which DoubleTake can't do. DoubleTake's last update, version 3.1.2, shipped on 10 March 2025.

Disclosure first: I build ContentCam, one of the two apps here, so I'm not neutral. Every factual claim about DoubleTake below comes from its own App Store listing and US App Store reviews, checked 5 November 2026. If something's out of date, email me and I'll correct it.

DoubleTake launched in January 2020 under Filmic. Filmic was acquired, and DoubleTake is now published by Bending Spoons (the seller is listed as Bending Spoons Operations SpA). The App Store listing shows version 3.1.2, a 3.4-star rating from 505 ratings, and a minimum of iOS 14.1.

Does DoubleTake work on iOS 26?

Not reliably, going by its App Store reviews. Of the 12 US reviews posted in 2026 (checked 5 November 2026), 9 report crashes, freezes, the app not opening, or the separate A and B files no longer saving. Three of them tie the problem directly to iOS 26:

  • 26 January 2026, 1 star: "No longer will save an a and b file since iOS 26".
  • 21 March 2026, 2 stars: an iPhone 13 Pro Max on iOS 26.3.1 can't get DoubleTake to open.
  • 25 March 2026, 2 stars: "After IOS 26 updates no longer opens", from a reviewer on a yearly plan.

Later reviews report the same on newer phones: on 22 August 2026 DoubleTake didn't get past its logo on an iPhone 16 Pro, and on 28 August 2026 it no longer opened on an iPhone 14 Pro. I haven't tested DoubleTake on iOS 26 myself. Version 3.1.2 predates iOS 26, so if you rely on DoubleTake, test a recording before an important shoot.

What can ContentCam do that DoubleTake can't?

DoubleTake records two cameras at once. Your wide lens and your telephoto, or the front and the back. That is what it's built for.

ContentCam records two cameras at once and two aspect ratios at once. Front & Back covers the reaction-plus-POV job. The dual-format mode captures one scene as a 9:16 vertical file and a 16:9 horizontal file in a single take, so the same footage is ready for TikTok and YouTube without filming twice. One 3-star DoubleTake review from 23 October 2025 asked for exactly that: recording landscape and portrait at the same time.

On top of that, ContentCam has a teleprompter, manual white balance (2500 to 8000K), ISO and shutter, 4K and up to 60fps with one camera, and an Android app.

What should you know about DoubleTake before a shoot?

1080p is the listed ceiling. The listing states 1080p at high bitrate encoding. A 1-star review from 6 September 2026 says DoubleTake shot 1080p even with 4K selected. If you crop or reframe in the edit, starting at 1080p costs you room you can't get back.

The multicam device list ends at the iPhone 13 series. Nothing newer is named, and the listing still says "Per iOS 15 capabilities".

Recordings can be lost in a crash. Reviews from 29 August 2025 and 1 January 2026 describe DoubleTake crashing mid-recording and losing the clip.

How much does DoubleTake cost?

DoubleTake is free to download with in-app purchases, all of them subscriptions. On 5 November 2026 the App Store listing showed weekly options from $0.99 to $9.99 and yearly options from $19.99 to $99.99, across several bundles. None of the listed purchases is one-time. Four US reviews since March 2025 complain about the billing: weekly plans, a free trial that led to a yearly charge, and no lifetime option.

ContentCam has monthly and annual subscriptions (the iPhone annual plan has a 7-day free trial) and a one-time lifetime purchase, all with the same features. The free tier records both formats in HD with no ads and no watermark. Current pricing is here.
