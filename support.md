## Devices & compatibility Which iPhones does ContentCam support?

iOS 18 or later, so iPhone XS (2018) and newer. Front & Back mode works on all of them, including iPhone 15 and iPhone 16, which don't have Apple's Dual Capture. Dual Lens mode needs an ultra-wide camera, so iPhone 11 or later (not iPhone SE or 16e). Does ContentCam work on Android?

Yes. [ContentCam for Android](https://contentcam.app/android/) is on Google Play and records portrait and landscape at the same time, with Front & Back mode, the teleprompter, manual white balance, ISO and shutter, timelapse, and 4K and 60fps where your phone's camera supports them. Dual Lens mode is iPhone only for now. Which phones support 4K and 60fps?

It depends on the camera. ContentCam checks what your phone's active lens can record and only offers what it can sustain, so you never pick a mode that quietly fails. On supported iPhones, 4K works in all three modes: Single Lens at up to 60fps, Dual Lens, and Front & Back with both cameras (from iOS 2.9).

## Recording What's free, and what needs Premium?

Free: unlimited Single Lens recording in portrait and landscape at once, in 1080p at up to 30fps, with HDR, cinematic stabilisation (on iPhone), the front camera, 3 projects, and 5 free Dual Lens and 5 free Front & Back recordings. No watermark, no ads and no account, ever.

Premium adds 4K, 60fps, unlimited Dual Lens and Front & Back, manual controls, the teleprompter, timelapse, Apple Log, Combine and unlimited projects. Is there a watermark?

No. Never, on any tier. What frame rates does ContentCam record?

24, 30 or 60fps, plus 25 and 50fps (PAL) once you turn them on in Settings, on phones whose camera offers them. The free version records up to 30fps; 60fps is Premium. Is the landscape just a crop of the portrait?

No. Single Lens records the camera's full 4:3 frame and cuts each format from it, so the landscape keeps the full width and the portrait keeps the full height. Each file is encoded on its own in HEVC, at 1080×1920 and 1920×1080 or the 4K equivalents. Dual Lens (iPhone 11 and later) goes further: the wide and ultra-wide cameras record at the same time, so each format gets its own framing. [More on the modes](https://contentcam.app/features/dual-camera/). Can I record the front and back cameras at the same time?

Yes, with Front & Back mode, on iPhone and Android. It saves the selfie camera and the rear camera as two separate files. Combine can then turn them into one picture-in-picture or split-screen video, keeping the originals. Trying Combine layouts is free; saving one is Premium. Does ContentCam support HDR and Apple Log?

HDR is a single toggle on iPhone, on the free version. Apple Log (from iOS 2.9) records flatter footage for colour grading, on iPhone 15 Pro and newer Pro models, with Premium. What happens if a call or alarm interrupts my recording?

Recording carries on through notifications, alarms, Siri and auto-lock, and keeps rolling during phone calls, though the audio has a gap for the length of the call. If the app is force-quit mid-take, the recording is recovered the next time you open it. Does the teleprompter scroll automatically?

Yes. It scrolls hands-free at a speed you set, with adjustable text size, colour, reading-line position and a mirror mode for reflector rigs. iPhone and Android, Premium. [Details here](https://contentcam.app/features/teleprompter/).

## Files & storage Where are my recordings saved?

In the app's own storage, organised by project and scene, and you can browse them in the Files app under On My iPhone › ContentCam. Turn on Save to Photos and every saved take also goes to your photo library. Files are named like my_project_scene_001_Portrait.mov, so you can tell them apart in an editor. A recording isn't in Photos. Where is it?

Open the project in ContentCam, or look in Files › On My iPhone › ContentCam. If Save to Photos is off, takes stay in the app until you export them. Don't delete the app while you look: deleting it deletes anything it's holding. [Full guide to finding a missing video](https://contentcam.app/blog/iphone-video-not-in-photos-after-recording/). Does ContentCam need access to my whole photo library?

No. It only asks to add videos to Photos, so it can't see your existing photos and videos. Can I trim a take before saving?

Yes. Trim is built into the review screen; applying a trim is Premium.

## Purchases How much does Premium cost?

Three ways to buy it: an annual plan with a 7-day free trial, a monthly plan, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Same features on every plan. Prices are set per region, so the App Store and Google Play show the current price in your currency. [Full breakdown](https://contentcam.app/pricing/). Can I try Premium features before paying?

Yes. Everything can be previewed: the manual dials move, the teleprompter opens, 4K toggles on. The paywall only appears when you press record with a Premium feature switched on. Dual Lens and Front & Back also give you 5 free recordings each. If I buy lifetime, is it really for life?

Yes. A lifetime purchase stays a lifetime purchase, including every future update. If anything ever changes about how ContentCam is sold, lifetime buyers keep what they paid for. How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

On iPhone: Settings › Upgrade to Premium › Restore Purchases. On Android: Settings › Premium › Restore. Use the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. If it still doesn't unlock, email me your order ID and I'll sort it out. I bought on iPhone. Can I use Premium on Android (or the other way)?

Purchases are tied to the store you bought from. If you bought lifetime and switch platforms, email me your order ID and I'll help you move over. How do I cancel my subscription?

Through Apple or Google, not the app. iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Android: Google Play › profile › Payments & subscriptions. You keep Premium until the period ends. How do I get a refund?

iPhone: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). Android: your Google Play order history. Stuck? Email me and I'll point you the right way. I have an offer code. Where do I redeem it?

iPhone: Settings › Upgrade to Premium › Redeem Code.

Your phone model and what you were recording help me fix it faster. How often is ContentCam updated?

Every week or two. Every iPhone and Android version, with what changed, is listed in the [changelog](https://contentcam.app/changelog/).

## Privacy Does ContentCam collect my data?

Your videos never leave your phone and there is no account. The Android app collects nothing. From version 2.8.1, the iPhone app measures which ads lead to installs, trials and purchases, using Apple AdServices and the Meta SDK (in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, Meta only if you allow tracking). Recording works offline. Three permissions only: Camera, Microphone and Photo Library (add-only). [Full privacy policy](https://contentcam.app/privacy).
