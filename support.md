# ContentCam Support & FAQ: Get Help

Source: https://contentcam.app/support/

Answers to common ContentCam questions: device compatibility, dual-lens recording, 4K, file locations, subscriptions, refunds and more. Email support included.

ContentCam is built by one developer, and I read every message. If your question isn't answered below, email [contentcam.app@gmail.com](mailto:contentcam.app@gmail.com) or ask in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/YxUpbUgthH).

## Devices & compatibility

### Which iPhones does ContentCam support?

iOS 18 or later, so iPhone XS (2018) and newer. Front & Back mode works on all of them, including iPhone 15 and iPhone 16, which don't have Apple's Dual Capture. Dual Lens mode needs iPhone 11 or later, because it needs the ultra-wide camera, which iPhone 11 introduced.

### Does ContentCam work on Android?

Yes. [ContentCam for Android](https://contentcam.app/android/) is on Google Play and records portrait and landscape simultaneously, with manual white balance, ISO and shutter controls, timelapse, and 4K on supported phones. Dual Lens mode is iPhone-only for now; the teleprompter is on both.

### Which phones support 4K and 60fps?

Depends on the camera hardware. ContentCam checks what your phone's active lens can actually deliver and only offers what it can sustain, so you'll never select a mode that silently fails.

## Recording

### What frame rates does ContentCam record?

24, 30 or 60 fps, plus 25 fps on Android phones whose camera supports it, depending on what the active lens supports. 60 fps is a premium feature; the free tier records at 30 fps.

### Does ContentCam support HDR video?

On iPhone, yes: a single toggle, on the free tier. Android doesn't have HDR recording yet.

### Does recording two formats at once reduce quality?

No. Both files are encoded independently in HEVC: portrait at 1080×1920, landscape at 1920×1080, or 4K equivalents with premium. Neither file is an upscaled crop of the other.

### What's the difference between Single Lens and Dual Lens mode?

Single Lens records one camera at its native 4:3 sensor shape, then crops a 9:16 and a 16:9 out of that frame. You keep the full horizontal view in landscape and the full vertical in portrait. Dual Lens (iPhone 11+) records the wide and ultra-wide cameras at the same time, so the two files have genuinely different framing. [Full explanation here](https://contentcam.app/features/dual-camera/).

### Why is 4K only available in Single Lens mode?

iPhone hardware can't sustain 4K from two cameras at once. Single Lens records 4K; Dual Lens runs at 1080p.

### Is there a watermark?

No. Never, on any tier.

### What happens if a call or alarm interrupts my recording?

Recording continues through notifications, alarms, Siri and auto-lock, and keeps rolling during phone calls, though the audio track gaps for the length of the call. If the app is force-quit mid-take, the interrupted recording is recovered next launch.

### Does the teleprompter scroll automatically?

Yes. It scrolls hands-free at a speed you set, with adjustable text size, colour, reading-line position and a mirror mode for reflector rigs. iPhone and Android, premium. [Details here](https://contentcam.app/features/teleprompter/).

## Files & storage

### Where are my recordings stored?

In the app's own storage, organised by project and scene, and browsable in the iOS Files app under ContentCam. Turn on Save to Photos and every saved take also lands in your photo library. Files are auto-named like my_project_scene_001_Portrait.mov so you can tell them apart in an editor.

### Can I trim a take before saving?

Yes, trim on save is built into the review screen (premium to apply the trim).

## Purchases

### How much does Premium cost?

Three ways to buy it: an annual plan with a free trial, a rolling monthly plan, or a one-off lifetime purchase. Same features on every plan. Pricing is set per region, so the App Store and Google Play listings show the current price in your own currency. [Full breakdown](https://contentcam.app/pricing/).

### Can I try premium features before paying?

Yes. Everything is previewable: manual dials move, the teleprompter opens, 4K toggles. The paywall appears only when you record with a premium feature active.

### How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

On iPhone: Settings → Upgrade to Premium → Restore Purchases. On Android: Settings → Premium → Restore. Sign in with the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Through Apple or Google, not the app. iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions. You keep premium until the period ends.

### How do I get a refund?

iOS: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). Android: Google Play order history. Stuck? Email me and I'll point you the right way.

### I have an offer code. Where do I redeem it?

iPhone: Settings → Upgrade to Premium → Redeem Code.

## Help & bugs

### Something isn't working. How do I report it?

First update to the latest version from the App Store or Google Play, as many fixes are already out. If it still happens, tell me through the app's Settings, email [contentcam.app@gmail.com](mailto:contentcam.app@gmail.com), or message me in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/YxUpbUgthH). Your phone model and what you were recording help me fix it faster.

### Can I combine Front & Back into one video?

Yes. Front & Back saves two separate files, and you can also combine them into one video, picture-in-picture or split screen. It's part of Premium on iPhone and Android.

## Privacy

### Does ContentCam collect my data?

Your videos never leave your phone and there is no account. The Android app collects nothing. From version 2.8.1, the iPhone app measures which ads lead to installs, trials and purchases, using Apple AdServices and the Meta SDK (in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, Meta only if you allow tracking). Recording works offline. Three permissions only: Camera, Microphone, Photo Library (add-only). [Full privacy policy](https://contentcam.app/privacy).
