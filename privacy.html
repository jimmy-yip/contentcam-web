Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 July 2026

ContentCam is built by an Australian solo developer for content creators who shoot both portrait and landscape video. The app is designed to collect no personal data, send no analytics, and require no account.

Data we collect

None. The app does not request, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information. There is no account, no login, no telemetry, no third-party SDKs.

Permissions

ContentCam requests three iOS permissions, all required for its core function:

  • Camera — to record video.
  • Microphone — to record audio with your video.
  • Photo Library (add only) — to save your recorded videos to your Photos library. The app does not request read access to your existing photo library; it can only add new videos that you record in the app.

All three are explained in iOS's permission prompts. You can revoke them at any time in iOS Settings → ContentCam.

Data we don't collect

  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, calendar, browsing history, or any other personal data.
  • We do not have access to your existing photo library — only the ability to add new videos that you record in the app.

Where your videos are stored

  • Inside the app's sandboxed Documents directory until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • In your iOS Photos library when you choose to save a take from the in-app review screen. ContentCam does not delete videos from your Photos library; that's managed by you in the iOS Photos app.

Third-party services

None. ContentCam does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics services, ad networks, or backend servers. It is a fully on-device app.

Purchases and subscriptions

ContentCam offers optional in-app purchases and subscriptions. All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store — ContentCam never sees or stores your payment details. We receive only anonymised, aggregated sales data from Apple (such as how many subscriptions were sold), never anything that identifies you. Your subscription is managed entirely through your Apple Account in iOS Settings.

Children

ContentCam is rated 4+ on the App Store and is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Contact

For questions about this policy, email contentcam.app@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy (e.g., adding cloud sync or accounts in a future version), we'll update this page and notify users via App Store release notes.
