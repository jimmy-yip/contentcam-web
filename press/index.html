The facts

What it isA camera app that records 9:16 portrait and 16:9 landscape video simultaneously from a single take.
One-line descriptionContentCam records portrait and landscape video at the same time, so creators publish to TikTok and YouTube from one take.
PlatformsiOS (iPhone XS and later, iOS 18+) and Android. App Store name: "ContentCam: Dual Camera Video".
LaunchedMay 2026 on the App Store; Android followed on Google Play.
DeveloperJimmy Yip, solo independent developer, Sydney, Australia.
Headline featuresSimultaneous dual-format capture (Single Lens full-FOV or Dual Lens two-camera), built-in teleprompter, manual controls (EV, white balance 2500–8000K, ISO, shutter), timelapse, 4K, HDR.
Business modelFree with premium upgrade: US$19.99/year (7-day trial), US$4.99/month, or US$39.99 lifetime. No ads.
PrivacyZero data collection. No accounts, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. Works fully offline.
LinksApp Store · Google Play · Instagram · TikTok · YouTube · Discord

Why it exists (the short version)

Creators who publish to both vertical platforms (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) and YouTube either film everything twice or crop one format out of the other and lose framing. ContentCam records the sensor's full 4:3 frame and cuts both deliverables from it, so the landscape file keeps its full width and the portrait keeps its full height. On iPhone 11+ it can also record two rear cameras simultaneously for genuinely different framings.
