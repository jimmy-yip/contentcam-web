## Frequently asked questions Can I try Premium before paying?

Yes. The Premium camera features work in the live preview, and the paywall only appears when you press record with one switched on. Dual Lens and Front & Back give you 5 free recordings each, and the annual plan starts with a 7-day free trial. What happens when the free trial ends?

After 7 days the annual plan starts and you're billed for the year, unless you cancel before then in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions. Cancel during the trial and you pay nothing. On iPhone, Apple sends you a reminder before the trial ends. What happens if I cancel my subscription?

You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for, then the app goes back to the free tier. Your recordings and projects stay where they are. Lifetime or subscription: which should I pick?

All three plans unlock the same features, including every new feature that comes out. Lifetime is one payment with nothing to renew. Annual costs much less per month than monthly and is the only plan with a free trial. Monthly suits a single project: use it while you need it, then cancel. Does Premium work with Family Sharing?

On iPhone, the lifetime purchase includes Family Sharing for up to 6 people. Does my purchase carry over between iPhone and Android?

Purchases belong to the Apple or Google account you bought with, so they come with you to any new phone on the same store: tap Restore Purchases in Settings. They don't move between the App Store and Google Play by themselves. Where do I see the price?

In the app, or on the App Store and Google Play listings. Prices are set for each country in your local currency, so one number here would be wrong for most people. You always see the exact price before you confirm. Are there ads or a watermark on the free version?

No. There are no ads and no watermark on any plan, free included, and no account needed.
