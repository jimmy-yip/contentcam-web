ContentCam for Android

The same core idea as the iPhone app: record once, get a 9:16 portrait file and a 16:9 landscape file from the same take. Native Android app, built on the same full-field-of-view capture approach, on Google Play now.

What the Android app does

Dual-format recording

One take produces both files, cropped from the sensor's full 4:3 frame so landscape keeps its full width. Rear and front cameras.

Manual controls

White balance temperature and tint dials, plus fully manual ISO and shutter speed on phones whose cameras support it.

Timelapse, 4K, 60fps

Timelapse with adjustable speed. 4K and 60fps where the hardware genuinely delivers them; the app checks rather than guessing.

Ultra-wide 0.5×

On phones that expose an ultra-wide camera, a 0.5× stop gives both crops the wider field of view.

Shoot sideways, get upright files

Hold the phone rotated and both saved files are tagged with the right orientation, so they play upright in your gallery, TikTok, CapCut or Premiere without a manual rotate.

Zero data collection

The Android app collects no data: no account, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. The Play Store Data Safety label reads "no data collected" because none is.

Android questions

Which Android phones work best?

Any reasonably modern phone records dual-format 1080p. Manual exposure, 4K and ultra-wide depend on what the manufacturer exposes; recent Samsung Galaxy and Pixel phones support the most.

Is the Android app a port or a separate app?

Native Kotlin, built specifically for Android's camera stack. Same recording concept, not a wrapper.

Is the teleprompter on Android?

Yes, it's already there. Android got it in 2.0, with the same script box, speed, size, colour and mirror mode as iPhone.

Is ContentCam the same app as DualShot Recorder or OneTake?

No. ContentCam is made by Jimmy Yip, and on Google Play it's listed as ContentCam: Dual Camera Video, package name app.contentcam.android. DualShot Recorder, OneTake and other dual-format apps with similar names come from different developers and aren't connected to ContentCam. If a Play listing doesn't say "Jimmy Yip" as the developer, it isn't ContentCam.
