# A teleprompter inside the camera

ContentCam's Built-in Teleprompter puts your script on screen and scrolls it hands-free while you record. Write or paste the script, hit record, read. No second app, no second device, no memorising.

## Why built-in beats a separate teleprompter app

Separate teleprompter apps have one structural problem: they aren't the camera. You either film through their (usually worse) camera, or juggle two apps and record your screen. And none of them give you a portrait and a landscape file from the take.

In ContentCam the script scrolls over the live camera view, close to the lens, so your eyes stay near the glass. When you finish the take you have your lines delivered once, captured in both 9:16 and 16:9.

## What you can adjust

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Scroll speed** | Match the scroll to your natural talking pace. Slow it down for dense scripts, speed it up for energy. |
| **Text size** | Bigger text is readable at arm's length; smaller fits more of the script on screen. |
| **Text colour** | 8 colours, so the script stays readable over bright skies or dark rooms. |
| **Reading line** | Choose where the current line sits: position 1, 2 or 3 from the top. Higher keeps your eyes closer to the lens. |
| **Mirror mode** | Horizontal flip for teleprompter reflector rigs, where the phone's screen bounces off angled glass. |

## Teleprompter questions

**Does the iPhone Camera app have a teleprompter?** No. The stock Camera app has no teleprompter, and neither does the Photos app. You either read from a second device propped behind the phone, or use a camera app with one built in, which is what ContentCam does: the script scrolls over the shot you are actually recording, so your eyes stay near the lens.

### Does it scroll automatically while recording?

Yes. It starts scrolling when you record and runs hands-free at the speed you set.

### Can I use it with the front camera?

Yes, and that's how most people use it: talking-head videos where you read while facing the lens.

### Does the script appear in my video?

No. The teleprompter is an overlay on your screen only. The recorded files are clean.
