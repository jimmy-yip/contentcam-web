# Manual Camera Controls for Video: ISO, Shutter, WB | ContentCam

Source: https://contentcam.app/features/manual-controls/

ContentCam gives you manual exposure, white balance (2500–8000K), ISO and shutter speed for video on iPhone and Android. Preview free, settings persist between takes.

Auto exposure pumps when someone walks past a window. Auto white balance drifts mid-take and your footage changes colour between cuts. ContentCam's manual panel exists for exactly those moments.

## The controls

| Control | Range | When you'd use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Exposure (EV bias)** | Dial around auto | Scene reads too bright or too dark, but you still want auto doing the work. |
| **White balance** | 2500K – 8000K | Lock colour temperature so mixed lighting (window + lamp) stops shifting between takes. 2700K for warm indoor light, ~5500K for daylight, higher for shade. |
| **ISO** | Manual wheel, hardware range | Pin sensitivity to control noise instead of letting auto crank it in low light. |
| **Shutter speed** | Manual wheel, hardware range | Hold the 180° rule (1/60 at 30fps) for natural motion blur, or go faster to freeze action. |

Plus the basics: tap to focus, drag to expose, pinch to zoom, 0.5×/1×/2× lens switching, and torch. All manual settings apply to both output files, portrait and landscape.

## Questions

### Do manual settings affect both output files?

Yes. Exposure, white balance, ISO and shutter apply to the capture itself, so the portrait and landscape files match exactly.

### Can I use manual controls with the teleprompter?

Yes, they're independent. Lock your exposure, load your script, record.

### What's the 180° shutter rule?

Set shutter speed to roughly double your frame rate (1/60 at 30fps) for the motion blur people expect from film. [Full explainer here](https://contentcam.app/blog/iphone-manual-camera-controls-video/).
