Features

ContentCam records 9:16 portrait and 16:9 landscape video simultaneously from one take. Around that core sits a set of tools built for people who publish, not just record.

Dual-format recording

Single Lens mode crops both formats from the native 4:3 sensor for full field of view. Dual Lens mode records the wide and ultra-wide cameras at once. The core of the app.

Front / Back mode

Record the front and rear cameras at the same time: your reaction and your POV in a single take. Reaction-style content without a second phone. iPhone and Android; premium, with 5 free recordings to try.

Built-in Teleprompter

Script on screen, scrolling hands-free while you record. Speed, size, colour, reading-line position, mirror mode. New in 2.0, iPhone.

Manual controls

Exposure bias, white balance from 2500K to 8000K, manual ISO and shutter speed. Settings persist between sessions if you want.

Timelapse

Dual-format timelapse. Set the phone down, come back, get a portrait and a landscape timelapse from the same session. Premium.

4K, 60fps, HDR

1080p HEVC by default. 4K and 60fps with premium, where your phone's hardware supports them. HDR video is free.

Stabilisation & recovery

Stabilised by default. Recordings survive calls, alarms and notifications; interrupted takes are recovered on next launch.

Creator workflow

Project / Scene / Take organisation with automatic file naming like my_project_scene_001_Portrait.mov. Side-by-side review after every take. Trim on save. Files app access.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, fully offline. The policy is one page.

See it in action

Free to download on both platforms.
