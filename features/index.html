Two formats, one take

A vertical and a horizontal video from every take

Single Lens mode records the camera's full 4:3 frame and saves a 9:16 and a 16:9 from it. The vertical keeps the full height, the horizontal keeps the full width, and each one is its own file.

  • Vertical for TikTok, Reels and Shorts and horizontal for YouTube, every time you press record
  • HD or 4K, set separately for each format
  • Portrait only when you don't need the horizontal video

Recording modes

Many ways to record

Switch modes from the bar at the bottom of the camera screen.

  • Single Lens: portrait and landscape from the full sensor, neither a crop of the other
  • Dual Lens: wide and ultra-wide at once, for two different perspectives. iPhone 11 or later.
  • Front & Back: your reaction and the scene in one take, as two files or combined into one video, picture-in-picture or split screen
  • Front camera in both formats, for talking to camera
  • Photo mode: a 9:16 and a 16:9 photo in one tap
  • Timelapse, 10x to 900x

Pro camera controls

Shoot like a pro

Leave everything on auto, or set it yourself and lock it so the shot doesn't drift mid-take.

  • HD or 4K at 24, 25, 30, 50 or 60fps
  • Manual exposure, white balance, ISO and shutter
  • Tap to focus, hold for AE/AF lock
  • Stabilisation, plus HDR video on iPhone
  • Background blur on Android, in beta
  • Grid, level and audio meter

On Android, 4K, 60fps and manual ISO and shutter depend on what your phone's camera supports. The app checks and only offers what it can record.

Teleprompter

Read your script while you record

Paste up to 15,000 characters and it scrolls over the camera view at the speed you set, so your eyes stay near the lens.

  • Speed, text size and colour, and where the reading line sits
  • Mirror mode for teleprompter rigs

Projects and takes

Stay organised

Every take is filed by project and scene and named for you, so you're not digging through your camera roll for the right version.

  • Projects, scenes and takes, with files named like my_project_scene_001_Portrait.mov
  • Review both videos side by side after every take, and trim before you save
  • Pause and carry on in the same take
  • Pick the folder your recordings go into
  • Interrupted takes recovered next time you open the app
